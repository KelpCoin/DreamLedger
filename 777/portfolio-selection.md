## Portfolio probability law

For illustrative independent probabilities:

P(at least one verified transaction) = 1 - product(1 - p_i)

This is not a forecast. Candidate outcomes are correlated, many candidates may have p_i approximately zero until discovery and buyer access exist, and one surface can cannibalize another.

Therefore the engine optimizes qualified economic encounters and verified outcomes, not page count.
