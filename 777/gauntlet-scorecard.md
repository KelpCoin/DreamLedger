# 777 Gauntlet Scorecard

Status: UNVERIFIED

The Gauntlet is the adversarial selection boundary for Elohim output. It does not create economic truth and it does not authorize external action.

Score each dimension 0-5:

1. Evidence quality
2. Buyer specificity
3. Economic gravity
4. Outcome clarity
5. Time compression
6. Risk reversal
7. Proof density
8. Cost of inaction
9. Fulfillment feasibility
10. Truth-boundary compliance

Selection rules:

- Any fabricated evidence, buyer, payment, outcome, or testimonial: DROP.
- Any candidate without a specific affected party and measurable friction: REFINE.
- Any candidate that requires a new ledger, queue, orchestrator, or truth system: DROP.
- Any candidate that conflicts with silo boundaries: DROP.
- A high score permits progression to the existing Authority Gate only.
- Authority Gate approval is still required before external effect.
- Telemetry, not Elohim preference, determines which candidates receive more substrate.
- Winners are refined. Weak candidates are dropped. New candidates are seeded from better economic substrate.

Economic truth remains:

REAL DEMAND -> REAL BUYER -> AUTHORIZED ACTION -> EXTERNAL RESULT -> SETTLED PAYMENT -> FULFILLMENT -> EVIDENCE -> VERIFIED.
