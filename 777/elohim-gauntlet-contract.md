# 777 Elohim → Gauntlet Contract

Elohim creates, refines, repairs, generates variants, identifies missing evidence, and proposes scorecards from accepted economic substrate.

Elohim may not certify truth, authorize external effects, declare revenue, mark buyers verified, or mutate the authoritative economic ledger.

The Gauntlet judges every Elohim candidate before promotion. It scores evidence quality, buyer specificity, economic gravity, outcome clarity, time compression, risk reversal, proof density, cost of inaction, fulfillment feasibility, and truth-boundary compliance.

Hard DROP: fabricated buyer/evidence/payment/outcome/testimonial; undefined affected party; no measurable economic friction; silo contamination; parallel ledger/queue/orchestrator/truth system; Authority Gate bypass.

A pass only permits the existing Authority Gate and an external test. It is not economic verification.

Telemetry allocates substrate:
- weak candidate: DROP or FREEZE
- useful response signal: REFINE
- strong external response/payment: WINNER
- winner: allocate more substrate and generate the next 5-10 variants

Public economic truth: Truth Oracle.
Internal authoritative truth: immutable BECK ledgers.

Economic chain:
REAL DEMAND → REAL BUYER → AUTHORIZED ACTION → EXTERNAL RESULT → SETTLED PAYMENT → FULFILLMENT → EVIDENCE → VERIFIED.
