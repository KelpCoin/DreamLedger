## Offer

We assess one AI-driven financial workflow and identify where
authorization, evidence, cross-system state, and auditability can fail.

The output is a concise control-gap report with remediation priorities.

## Deliverable

For one workflow:

1. Current agent/system chain
2. Where financial state crosses system boundaries
3. What each platform logs vs what is actually proven
4. Authorization gaps
5. Evidence gaps
6. Cross-system state transition risk
7. Auditability weaknesses
8. Named risk owner
9. Remediation priorities
10. Optional recommended control gate

Example chain:

Salesforce agent: quote approved
        |
        v
Oracle agent: revenue recognized
        |
        v
SAP agent: payment scheduled
        |
        v
Treasury/payment: money moves

The report asks:

Can the company independently prove the whole chain was legitimate —
not just that each system logged its own action?
