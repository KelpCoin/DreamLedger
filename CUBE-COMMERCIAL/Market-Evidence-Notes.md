# Market Evidence Notes — Agentic Cross-System Finance

STATUS: RESEARCH ONLY
REVENUE: NZ$0

## Strongest Public Signals

PwC:
- Agent-driven transactions cross platform boundaries.
- Platform controls do not cross platform boundaries.
- Salesforce / Oracle / SAP / Workday example.
- End-to-end traceability required.

SAP:
- Joule coordinating agents across finance, procurement, supply chain.
- Autonomous spend management becoming operational.
- AP workflows can post into S/4HANA with minimal human intervention.

JPMorgan:
- Autonomous treasury scenario involving substantial sums.
- Governance and accountability described as still under construction.
- Audit logs, explanations and overrides mentioned.

IMF:
- Agentic payments require authorization, settlement, traceability,
  compliance and legal certainty.
- Probabilistic agent behaviour vs deterministic payment infrastructure.

Visa:
- Agent-specific tokens, authenticated user instructions, transaction
  controls and signals.
- Verification of agent authority being built.

Mastercard:
- Agent Pay for Machines.
- Credentialing, verifiable intent, permissioning, spending limits,
  transaction and settlement capabilities.

## Caution

These validate the problem category.
They do NOT validate CUBE specifically.
They do NOT prove willingness to pay.
