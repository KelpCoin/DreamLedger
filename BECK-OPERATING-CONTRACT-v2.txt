BECK Operating Contract

Purpose: keep production observable, verifiable, recoverable, and commercially pointed.

Truth rules:
1. Health, traffic, clicks, checkout availability, and intent are not revenue.
2. Revenue requires attributable live payment evidence.
3. Delivery and evidence follow payment.
4. Failed or stale checks create exceptions, not success claims.
5. Routine observation and evidence collection may run automatically.
6. Consequential public posting, spending, or irreversible commercial actions remain approval-gated unless separately authorized.

Core loop:
OBSERVE -> RECORD -> COMPARE -> DETECT -> EXPERIMENT -> SELL -> VERIFY -> LEARN -> REPEAT

Canonical nucleus: DreamLedger. Other workloads attach to the same commerce spine instead of creating parallel economic truth systems.

RA_000001: first verified stranger payment attributable to the DreamLedger Founding Tile offer. Until proven, verified revenue remains NZ$0.

Heartbeat: health, deployment SHA convergence, canonical Founding Tile offer, and live checkout doorway. Retain evidence as an artifact. Surface failures as a single open GitHub incident to avoid alert spam.
