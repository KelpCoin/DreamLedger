The Gauntlet.

A candidate does not become a commercial action merely because an AI generated it. The Gauntlet is the deterministic boundary that tests whether a candidate is complete, payable, provable, private, correctly siloed and still subject to human authority.

Commercial completenessOffer identity, buyer, problem, deliverable and delivery mechanism.
Economic validityPositive price, currency, payment adapter and explicit checkout route.
Human authorityApproval is required and checkout remains blocked until approval.
ProofabilityDelivery proof and verification rules must be explicit.
PrivacyPrivate material must be explicitly excluded from the candidate.
Silo integrityCross-silo contamination is rejected before execution.
CANDIDATE -> REQUIRED FIELDS -> PRICE -> APPROVAL -> CHECKOUT BLOCK -> PRIVACY -> SILO -> PAYMENT PATH -> PROOF -> PASS / FAIL
Hard boundary: a Gauntlet PASS is not revenue. It means the candidate satisfied the deterministic pre-publication contract. Revenue changes only after external payment evidence and the existing proof chain.
Public trust model: expose the decision boundary, not private credentials or internal operational tokens. The website can show what must be true without pretending that an unobserved execution happened.
