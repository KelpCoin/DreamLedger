# Enterprise gap + surface thesis (DOOH · QR · pull · arbitrage frame)

## Enterprise gap (one paragraph)
DreamLedger has strong *contracts* (well-known, agent-commerce, fail-closed revenue rules, Stripe buy router, master silo stencil) and weak *market density* (few peer listings, deploy lag on public HTML, incomplete seller admin). Closing the gap is operational: deploy everything on `main`, seed NZ inventory, instrument funnels, keep 0% peer fee while selling tools.

## Programmatic DOOH
Treat each screen as a surface_id → QR → HTTPS path → optional paid offer. Never count plays as revenue.

## Digital arbitrage (ethical)
Transparency and routing — help humans find fair NZD trades faster. Tools may educate on fees; do not brand the platform as a scalping engine.

## Philanthropy / altruism as advertising
Pull (paramagnetic): support products, local goodwill, safety mods, fairness of 0% peer fee. Proof required if claims are public.

## Canonical QR
One short HTTPS URL per intent. Prefer `/`, `/mtg`, `/buy/...`. Parameterize DOOH with `?s=`.

## Healthy stack
HTTP 200 on money and discovery paths · valid TLS · DNS to live service · CI that deploys `public/` and checks `/healthz`.
