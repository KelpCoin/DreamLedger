# Micro B2B fulfillment (NZ$5–10)

Low price → must be **fast** or margin dies on time.

## DOC-EXTRACT-50 · NZ$5
**Input:** customer email + link/upload note + what fields they need  
**Output email:** structured bullets or simple table of extracted fields + confidence notes  
**SLA:** within 4 hours waking NZ time if input clear  
**Template:**
```
Subject: Document extraction — DreamLedger

Here are the fields extracted from what you provided:

| Field | Value |
|------|-------|
| … | … |

Gaps / unclear: …
```

## SUPPLIER-CHECK-50 · NZ$5
**Input:** supplier name, website/domain, country  
**Output:** 6–10 bullet findings (domain age signals, presence, red flags, “not verified” honesty)  
**Not:** full KYC or legal advice

## EVIDENCE-PACKET-10 · NZ$10
**Input:** claim they want supported  
**Output:** ordered list of evidence items to collect + simple packet outline

## QUOTE-COMPARE-49 · NZ$49
**Input:** 2+ quotes (paste or PDF text)  
**Output:** table of line items / totals / gaps / questions to ask supplier

## Rule
If input is too thin, ask one clarifying email once — then deliver best-effort.  
Log complete only after send.
