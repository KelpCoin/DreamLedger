# MTG community research → DreamLedger leverage (2026-10)

## What the community actually wants

### Pain with Facebook groups (global + NZ)
- Posts **buried** in scroll; no real search/filter
- **Scams** / no recourse; admins can’t protect money
- **Ban / rule chaos** (price norms 70–80% TCG low; opaque mods)
- One-post rules, no bump spam → low visibility
- Toxicity and off-topic noise
- Pricing fights (TCG low vs mid vs Card Kingdom)

### Pain with big marketplaces
- **High fees** (eBay, historic TCGPlayer friction)
- Slow / painful **listing** of collections
- Cold, not local; hard **trade** (not only buy/sell)
- NZ: international shipping and USD pricing awkward

### What people ask for instead
| Want | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Low / zero success fees** | Keep more of the sale |
| **Structured listings** | Find cards, not comments |
| **Have / want lists + trade match** | Cardsphere-style trades |
| **Verified / accountable sellers** | Safety without FB drama |
| **Clear NZD + condition + city** | Local pickup + post |
| **Fast listing** | Binder → list without spreadsheet hell |
| **Commander tools** | EDH is the volume format |
| **Safer payments guidance** | Goods & Services, not Friends & Family |

## NZ substrate (your real competitive set)

| Group / channel | Scale (approx) | Notes |
|-----------------|----------------|-------|
| **NZ MTG Marketplace** (FB) | ~6.3k members | Long-running BST; one post rule; NZD required |
| **NZ MTG Buy/Sell/Trade** | ~2.2k | Pricing guides; timestamps for high end |
| **NZ MTG Bazaar** | ~1.5k | Auckland-weighted |
| **Trade Me MTG** | Large inventory | Casual success fees changed 2026; still generalist |

**Takeaway:** The “6,000-strong Facebook group” pain is **real and local**. Winning = searchable + 0% + account-backed sellers + NZ logistics — not another US TCGPlayer clone.

## Best fit for *this* system (DreamLedger)

Already aligned:
- **0% platform success fee** positioning
- **Buy without account** / **sell needs free account**
- **Commander diagnostic $29** (ignition product)
- **Seller profit audit $29** (fee education vs Trade Me/FB)
- **Stripe** for tools; peer cards can stay peer-settled
- **Agent commerce** discovery for future automated offer surfaces
- **RIVET B2B** for LGS / wholesale later

Highest leverage to build next (order):
1. **Listing template** — title, set, condition, foil, NZD, city, pickup/post, photos required
2. **Want / ISO board** — free account; searchable
3. **Have list** — paste decklist / CSV later; start with manual
4. **Seller verification badge** (optional paid or activity-based) for high-value
5. **NZ pricing helper** — show TCG USD × FX sketch + “your NZD ask”
6. **Local city filters** — AKL / WLG / CHC / online
7. **Safety checklist** on every deal flow (already on silo page)

## Monetise without taxing the cards

Earn on tools, not a cut of every single:
- Commander diagnostic / deck clinics
- Seller profit audit
- Optional **boosted listing** or verification (later)
- LGS RIVET kits
- Billboard / presence for stores

## Honest constraint

Liquidity is the moat. Product must be **good enough to post in NZ groups** as “list once here, searchable forever” while FB stays the attention channel until volume flips.
