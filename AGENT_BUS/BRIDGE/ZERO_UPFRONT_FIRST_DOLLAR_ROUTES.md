3. Fiverr: seller earnings are 80% of completed order value. Use only acquisition and transaction methods permitted by current Fiverr terms.
4. Skip Trade Me Services for now: official fee is NZ$89 per 90-day listing. Reconsider only after buyer demand justifies spend.
5. Lemon Squeezy backup: 5% + $0.50 base, possible 1.5% international transaction surcharge, and 1% per payout to non-US bank accounts via Stripe. Verify actual payout costs.

Revenue exit: independent buyer + settled live payment + correct order + fulfilled deliverable + delivery evidence + attribution. Listings, clicks, notifications and test purchases are not revenue.
