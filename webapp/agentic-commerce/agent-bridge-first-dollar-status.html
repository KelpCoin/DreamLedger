Long-running build contract

Each task is small enough for an automated expedition to complete, test, and leave evidence for. The order is deliberate: prove the money path before expanding the marketplace.

  1. Repair the existing toll contract. Align the live manifest with actual routes and status. Verify the expected method, authentication, and error responses without charging a customer.
  2. Prove quota safety. Test idempotency, replay, concurrent requests, expiry, revocation, and durable usage accounting. Keep paid execution disabled until the persistence layer is healthy.
  3. Prove settlement-to-delivery. Match an independent buyer, settled payment, SKU, entitlement, fulfilled job, and delivery receipt. Keep unmatched events unverified.
  4. Ship one reusable component. Package the validated request/response contract, tests, provenance, version, licence, example, and acceptance verifier as a reusable stencil.
  5. Expand the marketplace spine. Listings and search, provider verification, quote/request, authorized offer acceptance, payment, delivery evidence, then verified ratings and disputes.
  6. Offer GPU capacity safely. Only after measuring actual capacity: authenticated queued jobs, tenant isolation, hard token/time limits, quotas, cancellation, and cost receipts. Never expose a home machine, local ports, files, credentials, or unrestricted shell execution.
  7. Add programmatic digital-out-of-home. Model only owner-authorized inventory, booking windows, pricing, creative constraints, and proof-of-play. Do not imply access to third-party screens.

Admission rules for compounding assets

  • One artifact must solve a distinct reader or operator job, cite its sources, and link to the relevant existing commercial wall.
  • Every artifact carries a source/hash, observed time, confidence, licence/permission, silo, test, and rollback path.
  • Reject duplicates, filler, unsupported market numbers, fabricated proof, and pages made only to reach a word count.
  • A 777 no-op is valid when no admissible signal exists. The next expedition must address a concrete blocker rather than generate duplicate pages.
