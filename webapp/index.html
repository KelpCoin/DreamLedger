Build your comparison starter.

This creates a blank CSV template in your browser. Your entries are not uploaded by this tool.

Keep the decision fields consistent.

Use one row per supplier or offer. Enter known values, keep unknowns blank, and record the source for every number that could change the decision.

Suggested fields: supplier, source URL, quote date, currency, unit price, MOQ, lead time, Incoterms, freight, duties/taxes, packaging, exclusions, validity date, and decision notes.

The worksheet is a template, not procurement, legal, customs, or financial advice. Verify source documents before committing funds.

CSV includes structured supplier fields and a row for your comparison name and decision question. Nothing is sent to a server.
A stricter definition of done

Evidence before certainty.

A polished interface should never blur what the system knows, what it assumes, and what it has actually done.

What a published page means

A page or offer is visible. That alone does not establish buyer demand, successful payment, delivery, or independent verification.

What a verified outcome means

Source → authorized action → external result → settled payment → fulfillment → delivery evidence. Missing links stay explicitly unverified.
