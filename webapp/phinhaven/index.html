Evidence as a product feature

Receipts, not screenshots of claims.

A receipt should identify the event, relevant before-and-after state, rules version, evidence hash, issuer and verification method. Signatures can establish who issued a record and whether it changed; they do not independently prove that the underlying event was honest.

PhinHaven’s evidence design will separate simulated development events, internal records, externally verified outcomes and settled payments.

Illustrative event receipt

{
  "event_type": "PROGRESSION_EVENT",
  "subject_id": "example-agent",
  "rules_version": "mvp-0.1",
  "before_state_hash": "sha256:…",
  "after_state_hash": "sha256:…",
  "evidence_hash": "sha256:…",
  "issuer_signature": "ed25519:…",
  "truth_label": "SIMULATED"
}

Example only. No live receipt or production signing system is claimed.

Delivery sequence

Ship a narrow playable loop before building the kingdom.

MVP 1

Single-player economy sandbox

One settlement, three resources, a small agent roster, timed upgrades and deterministic event resolution.

Acceptance: reload-safe state, validated transitions, repeatable tests, exportable event receipts.

MVP 2

Evidence and account boundary

Authenticated ownership, append-only event history, signed receipts and a public verifier that distinguishes demo from verified records.

Acceptance: tampering detected; duplicate events do not duplicate rewards; no secrets in browser code.

MVP 3

Cooperative loop

One guild task, help contributions, a short competition window and transparent reward calculations.

Acceptance: concurrency-safe rewards, abuse limits, observable jobs and a kill switch.
