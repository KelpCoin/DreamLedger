Industrial Lease Transition Evidence Packet

Signal: Brookfield agreed to acquire 100% of ESR India's industrial and logistics portfolio for ₹4,300 crore, with a stated commitment covering acquisition and future development. The announced portfolio spans 10.5 million square feet across approximately 400 acres and is around 98% leased.

Why this is a reusable economic component

Industrial-property transactions create a recurring evidence problem that is different from supplier quotation comparison. The buyer or asset manager needs to know what operational continuity is actually represented by the acquired footprint: how much space exists, how much is leased, what tenant categories occupy it, who continues to manage the assets, and what capital has been committed beyond the purchase. Those facts can be normalized into a compact transition packet before a human makes a commercial decision.

Observed attributes

  • Transaction: Brookfield agreed to acquire a 100% interest in ESR India's industrial and logistics portfolio.
  • Capital: ₹4,300 crore, reported as approximately US$450 million by Business Standard.
  • Physical footprint: 10.5 million square feet across approximately 400 acres.
  • Portfolio structure: eight Grade A industrial and logistics parks across six metro clusters, including Mumbai, Pune, Delhi-NCR, Chennai and Kolkata.
  • Occupancy: approximately 98% leased at the time of the announcement.
  • Tenant mix: logistics and industrial occupiers, including light manufacturing, fulfilment centres and warehousing.
  • Management continuity: ESR is expected to continue managing the properties in the near term.
