Parametric Risk-Transfer Backstop

777 primitive: PARAMETRIC-RISK-TRANSFER-BACKSTOP

Status: STRUCTURAL PRIMITIVE / COMMERCIAL HYPOTHESIS

Observed transaction

Algorithmic Insurance Services, operating as LIRG, provided US$15 million of quota-share reinsurance to New York-based MGA DLT Alert to support embedded parametric cyber warranty programs for cybersecurity solution providers. LIRG's media page records the arrangement as a March 2026 transaction, while DLT Alert's own publication describes the same US$15 million backing.

The supported warranties use predefined cyber-breach triggers tied to third-party technology vendors or suppliers. The structure is designed to turn verified incident telemetry into a defined compensation event rather than relying on a conventional loss-adjustment process.

Why this occupies new negative space

  • Capacity reservation: monetises scarce future production capacity. This primitive monetises balance-sheet risk capacity.
  • Take-or-pay: creates a buyer-backed demand floor. This primitive transfers contingent operational risk through a measurable trigger.
  • Forward-flow capital: funds recurring asset flows. This primitive attaches reinsurance capital to a trigger-defined liability stream.
  • Exclusive-rights-upfront: monetises bounded commercial rights. This primitive monetises protection against a specified adverse event.
  • Customer prepayment: moves customer cash forward against future delivery. This primitive moves risk-bearing capacity behind future contingent payouts.
