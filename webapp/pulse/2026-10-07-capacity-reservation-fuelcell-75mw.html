Capacity Reservation Signal

Observed entity: FuelCell Energy

Observed event: first Capacity Reservation Agreement with a major data center operator for a planned 75 MW Texas project.

Economic state: reservation payment → priority capacity lock → long-lead procurement → definitive project agreement → deployment → settlement.

What changed

FuelCell Energy disclosed that, after its third quarter, it signed its first Capacity Reservation Agreement with a major data center operator. The agreement covers a planned 75 MW project and includes an upfront reservation payment. In return, the operator receives priority access to FuelCell Energy manufacturing capacity while definitive project agreements are finalized. FuelCell Energy said it would reserve manufacturing capacity and begin procurement of long-lead components. Financial terms were not disclosed.

Why this is a reusable 777 component

This is not the same economic state as a completed sale, a financing facility, or a conventional purchase order. A customer is paying to secure future production capacity before the final project contract is complete. The durable primitive is therefore CAPACITY-RESERVATION: scarce future capacity becomes an economic asset that can be reserved, prioritized, and converted into downstream delivery.

The component can be applied across manufacturing, compute infrastructure, energy equipment, industrial machinery, logistics capacity, specialist services, and other markets where production or delivery slots are scarce. The key evidence fields are reservation payment present, capacity quantity, reservation priority, production window, long-lead commitment, definitive-contract state, and eventual delivery/payment state.
