Commercial acceptance gate

  1. Probe the route with a harmless, bounded request.
  2. Verify the exact product, currency, and amount at checkout.
  3. Observe a real independent settled payment with matching metadata.
  4. Prove idempotent fulfillment and deliver the promised output.
  5. Attach the evidence receipt to the service record before changing its status.

Current catalogue data marks settlement and fulfillment unverified. This page therefore makes no revenue claim.
