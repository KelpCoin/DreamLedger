777 sensory research artifact · 2026-10-07

Core proposition

An economic object should not be treated as a single document. A contract, invoice, company, receivable, supply chain or payment obligation is a latent state observed through multiple imperfect evidence channels.

The sensory primitive is:

BEAM → REGISTRATION → CONVERGENCE / DIVERGENCE → DELTA → ECONOMIC INTERPRETATION

Observation beams

  • Commitment: contracts, invoices, filings and stated obligations.
  • Operational: shipping, production, infrastructure, deployment and other observable footprints.
  • Human-capital: hiring velocity, role changes and organizational movement.
  • Market: pricing, counterparties, financing, competitive and commercial signals.
  • Temporal: event timing, state changes and velocity.

Critical rule

Do not average beams into a synthetic truth. Preserve each observation independently, register it to identity and time, then measure convergence and divergence. A disagreement is itself an observation.

Gauntlet divergence rule

A reconstruction cannot become VERIFIED merely because several sources agree. The Gauntlet must ask whether the sources are genuinely independent, correctly registered, temporally compatible and capable of producing an economic consequence.

Correlated echo ≠ independent confirmation.

Inference ≠ observation.

Divergence ≠ fraud.

Divergence becomes economically actionable only when it identifies a material discrepancy, an affected party, a plausible consequence and a testable action path.
