Reusable economic transition

ORIGINATED RECEIVABLES → ELIGIBILITY / REPRESENTATIONS → RECEIVABLES SALE → PURCHASE CONSIDERATION → ASSET TRANSFER → SERVICING / COLLECTION → SETTLEMENT EVIDENCE

The reusable dimension is different from forward-flow capital. Forward-flow attaches funding to a future stream or repeated asset flow. This component detects the monetisation of an already-originated receivable pool by transferring payment rights for purchase consideration. It is also different from customer prepayment: the cash comes from an asset purchaser acquiring an existing right to future payments, rather than from the customer advancing payment for future delivery.

  • TRANSFER_STATUS: sold, assigned, transferred, contributed or securitised.

  • SERVICING_PATH: who administers collection after transfer.
  • SETTLEMENT_EVIDENCE: filing, purchase agreement, transfer agreement, funding notice or transaction record.
