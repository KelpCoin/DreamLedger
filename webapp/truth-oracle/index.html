Evidence rules

A cheaper headline is not proof of savings.

Every published opportunity should distinguish VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED, STALE, CONTRADICTED, and TEST. Compare the total price and conditions. A listing, crawler result, or generated summary is a lead, not proof. No fake savings totals, fake reviews, or invented provider offers.
