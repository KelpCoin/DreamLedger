Before choosing

Quote-quality checklist

  • Same specification, quantity, service level, currency, and delivery location.
  • Include setup, shipping, duties, taxes, minimum order, transaction fees, and recurring charges.
  • Record validity date, lead time, warranty, support, exclusions, cancellation terms, and price-change clauses.
  • Ask for clarification where a line item is missing. Do not silently treat missing cost as zero.
  • Keep the original quote and note who confirmed it, when, and through which channel.

How to request comparable quotes

Send every supplier the same specification, volume, delivery location, required date, warranty/support expectation, and contract term. Request a line-item breakdown and validity period. Ask them to identify assumptions and exclusions explicitly.
