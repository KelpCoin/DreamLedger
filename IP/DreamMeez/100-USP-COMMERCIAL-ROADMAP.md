# DreamMeez execution priority from the 100-USP proposal

Canonical inventory: \`IP/DreamMeez/usp-register-100-20261028.json\`. All 100 concepts retained, but none of these entries alone establishes a deployed product, customer, license, artist-authorship proof or revenue.

## Gate 0: customer experience and independent first sale
Complete the existing DreamLedger storefront release, resolve the Vercel 403 deployment authorization and independently verify a paid NZ$49 supplier comparison with actual delivery. Preserve other BECK silos; do not put an unfinished avatar checkout ahead of a verified existing payment path. Measure path / -> offer -> checkout -> settled payment -> fulfilment without fabricated analytics.

## Gate 1: DreamMeez hand-drawn identity
Build a single complete, original, human-sketched avatar base plus three real cosmetic layers to prove the system can deliver art, not just show CSS preview concepts. Require original artist's source and SHA-256, license, transformed versions, accessible fit tests across small-to-very-large body builds, separate free try-on and paid entitlement, actual refund and entitlement tests. Run Peggy's art sign-off before release.

## Gate 2: first original paid cosmetic collection
Choose the easiest three candidates from the existing 12 (e.g. Glowcap Slippers, Cloudstep Lounge Pants, Halo Knot). Record hand-drawn masters, approved enhancement model, exactly what buyer receives, actual fulfilment workflow, and pricing tests. Publish only after explicit founder approval and all purchase claims are verified. No fake stock limits, no one-of-one rarity without database-backed allocation.

## Gate 3: social reach and recurring income
Hand-drawn stickers, shareable duo scenes, commissioned portraits with a real queue, a subscription only once monthly delivery is proven, and external artist submissions only with rights agreements and payout evidence. Treat "one costume per week" and grant promises as future hypotheses, not part of the existing supporter plans.

## Gate 4: marketplace and B2B
Artist revenue splits, white-label SDK, physical merchandise and licensing come after ownership, tax, fraud controls, usable exports and support capacity. Adult content must remain strictly siloed and age-gated; do not link it to a child-facing public avatar store.

## Claims not permitted yet
- "NZ$90 premium over AI-generated avatars" or "the price boundary is 8,000 JPY" lacks verified relevant methodology, comparable scope and evidence.
- "320M daily Bitmoji users" requires an up-to-date Bitmoji-specific source, not Snapchat users.
- "First hand-drawn avatar economy", "only platform", "under 24 hours sketch-to-shelf" and "human-made certified" are not publishable without independently verifiable evidence and genuine audit procedure.
- "Human wobble" can describe an artistic texture but cannot prove authorship. Hashes prove file integrity, not that a human drew it.
- "No AI training on user images" is a policy target, requiring actual provider contracts and system controls. "Air-gapped" is false if uploading or cloud model inference occurs.

## Measurable launch dashboard
Customer: landing visitor count, shop entry, avatar started, try-on interaction, offer viewed, checkout started, Stripe-paid, fulfilment-confirmed, refund/dispute. Each event needs a consent-aware privacy policy, server attribution and reconciliation; zero is not inferred from missing instrumentation.

Artist: original hash present, AI-assisted delta reviewed, asset slot compatibility, size-fit sweep, licensor and royalty terms, human final approval, count of original, shippable cosmetics.

LLM: per run model IDs, distinct-model count, roles, timestamps, input/output SHA-256, validation tests, PRs proposed and accepted, shipping outcomes. Pure scheduled GitHub tests do not count as LLM iterations.

Commercial: gross sales only from settled independent buyers, delivery evidence and net costs. Hypothesis scores and Stripe link creation are not revenue.

**No automatic public posts, direct messages, external promotion or surprise inference spending.** Every new public offer requires explicit Biggie and Peggy approval.
