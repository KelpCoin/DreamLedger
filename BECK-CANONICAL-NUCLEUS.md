# BECK Canonical Production Nucleus

This repository is the canonical production nucleus for the BECK control plane.

## Runtime rule
DreamLedger is the public commerce workload. Other MVPs are registered workloads and must remain silo-isolated.

## Commissioning rule
Production truth is established by runtime evidence, not by source-only claims.

## Economic rule
Revenue remains zero until attributable external payment evidence is verified. A paid event is not complete until the applicable fulfillment evidence is recorded.

## Control rule
Routine verification, reconciliation, evidence generation, and safe idempotent repair belong to the machine. Consequential public, financial, or irreversible actions remain approval-gated.

- Production version endpoint reports a live SHA different from the merged main commit.
- Public surface guard contains stale expectations from an older storefront contract.
