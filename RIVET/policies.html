Policies

Plain summary for buyers and sellers. Not a substitute for full legal terms where those exist.

Fees

  • Platform success fee on current public offers: 0%.
  • Stripe fees still apply to card payments.
  • Listing intake is free. Publication requires approval.

Listings

  • Submitted listings enter PENDING_APPROVAL.
  • Illegal or fraudulent content will not be published.
  • Sellers must be entitled to sell what they list.

Payments & revenue truth

  • Checkout via Stripe. Card details not stored on DreamLedger.
  • A started checkout is not a sale. Abandoned carts are not revenue.
  • Verified revenue requires external payment + fulfillment evidence.

Contact

Use the email provided at intake or checkout for listing or payment issues.
