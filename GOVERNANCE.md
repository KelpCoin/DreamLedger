# Governance

## Operating Principle

**AI proposes. Evidence decides.**

AI systems may propose ideas, actions, or conclusions. Evidence determines whether those proposals are accepted.

## Decision Gates

- **GREEN**: Strong evidence, low consequence -> proceed.
- **AMBER**: Plausible but consequential -> verify the smallest decisive fact first.
- **RED**: Unsupported, contradictory, fabricated, or high-consequence -> stop.

## Scope

This document governs human overrides and future agentic roles. It is a governance policy only and does not alter product code or production behavior.

No persona subsystem, override-loop engine, or policy-update engine is implied by this document.
