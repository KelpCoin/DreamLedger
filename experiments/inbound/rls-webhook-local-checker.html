Preliminary findings

Warnings are not audit findings without manual verification. A clean scan does not establish safety. For actual tenant isolation, test disjoint seeded tenants with a genuinely restricted role and authenticated principal binding.

Boundaries of this tool

FORCE RLS is not universally mandatory: it matters for table-owner access, and it does not override roles with BYPASSRLS. Published listings may be intentionally public. Webhook transaction safety cannot be established from keywords alone. Concurrency safety can use atomic UPDATE or other correct patterns, not just SELECT FOR UPDATE. Never execute generated SQL against production based on a static warning.

A professional diagnostic can include role and grant inspection, actual two-tenant read/write tests, Stripe signature and idempotency review, and controlled concurrent reservation tests. Paid assessment is not yet offered or linked here.
