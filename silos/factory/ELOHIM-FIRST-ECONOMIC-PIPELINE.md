## Canonical sequence

`CONVERSATION + SOURCES + TELEMETRY -> ELOHIM -> CUBE SWARM -> GAUNTLET -> HUMAN-AUTHORIZED TEST -> SETTLEMENT -> FULFILLMENT -> INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE -> TRUTH ORACLE REVIEW -> BECK LEDGER -> COMPOUNDING GATE -> CONTROLLED REPLICATION`

The 777 expedition is downstream of Elohim. Its outputs must be classified as evidence, a qualified economic lead, an audit component, a reusable artifact, or a no-op/negative result, then pass the applicable Gauntlet and human gates. It may not bypass Elohim, directly edit canonical Truth Oracle entries, activate checkout, or claim revenue.

## Stage contract

### Elohim
Read conversation-derived hypotheses and existing evidence. Deduplicate against products, PRs, schemas, and workflows. Record buyer, pain, alternatives, value mechanism, delivery, price hypothesis, costs, risks, falsification condition, and provenance. Label claims FACT, HYPOTHESIS, ESTIMATE, UNKNOWN, or VERIFIED OUTCOME. Never invent inventory, buyers, or market statistics.

### CUBE swarm
Explore 5-10 bounded variants only when the substrate can support them. Prefer cheap, high-information tests. No paid acquisition or external action without authorization. Track candidate IDs, lineage, duplicates, and negative results. Return comparative evidence, not an unranked flood of ideas.

### Gauntlet
Record `PASS_TO_BOUNDED_TEST`, `REPAIR_AND_RESUBMIT`, `QUARANTINE`, or `REJECT`, with reasons and evidence links. Test buyer specificity, independent demand, provenance, reproducibility, measured value, delivery quality/cost, price evidence, substitutes, licensing, privacy, liability, distribution compliance, test reversibility, attribution, and silo isolation. PASS permits proposing a bounded experiment only. It does not prove a business or authorize public launch.

### Offer and economic proof
Use one silo, one CTA, one commercial boundary per approved candidate. Keep catalog empty until supply is verified. Define digital deliverables and fulfillment evidence explicitly. Production payment, public release, spend, and irreversible actions require human approval. Verified commercial success requires an independent external buyer, settled payment, correct attribution, completed fulfillment, and retrievable delivery evidence. Tests, traffic, candidate counts, generated reports, and model outputs are not revenue.

### Compounding and replication
A reusable component needs a defined interface, provenance, dependencies/licence, tests or fixtures, reproduction steps, failure modes, and measured cost/time/quality. A later comparable run must show the change after adaptation and maintenance costs. Otherwise mark it `REUSABLE_CANDIDATE` or `UNSCORABLE`, not proven compounding.
