# Social Distribution Playbook

Status: APPROVAL-GATED

Goal: turn one verified buyer into additional qualified attention without automated spam.

## Content loop

1. Publish one useful, visually clear billboard example.
2. Use one CTA: own a 100x100 permanent block for NZ$29.
3. Show the permanent QR doorway.
4. Invite buyers to share their own block after purchase.
5. Capture exact buyer language and observed behavior.
6. Repeat only the formats that produce measurable qualified clicks or verified payments.

## Channel variants

### LinkedIn

Permanent digital billboard space for a one-time NZ$29. Built for small businesses, makers and creators who want a durable public placement rather than another recurring ad.

### Facebook

Own a small piece of a permanent digital billboard for NZ$29 once. Image + destination link, reviewed before placement.

### X / short-form

One-time digital billboard block. NZ$29. Your image + link. Permanent placement after review. One QR doorway.

### WeChat / Chinese-language adaptation

Use a native-language human translation before publishing. Do not machine-spam groups or claim local traction that has not been observed.

### WhatsApp / Discord / Slack

Use only in communities where promotion is allowed and only when the sender has permission to post.

## Virality mechanics

- Buyer share card.
- Public block URL.
- Permanent QR doorway.
- Referral experiment after first verified sale.
- Screenshot-ready placement proof.
- Local-language variants after evidence of demand.

## Approval gate

Nothing in this file authorizes autonomous posting. Human approval is required for every external publication.

## Measurement

Track: impressions when available, qualified clicks, checkout starts, paid transactions, refunds, and buyer referrals. CI/CD activity is never a substitute for commercial evidence.
