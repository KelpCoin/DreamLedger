## Purpose

Turn the specialist swarm into one controlled production loop spanning discovery, creation, packaging, criticism, human relevance, monetization, distribution preparation, accounting, audit, representation, adversarial control and truth verification.

The swarm is not a collection of autonomous personalities. It is a staged control plane with durable evidence and authority boundaries.

## Four authority classes

A0 OBSERVE
Read public information, inspect internal state, identify opportunities, refresh evidence.

A1 INTERNAL
Create drafts, code, datasets, analyses, offers, tests, reconciliations, artifacts and internal queue state.

A2 PREPARE
Prepare external-ready work, human-review packets, proposals, fulfillment packages and publication candidates. No external commitment.

A3 EXTERNAL
Submit, publish, spend, accept, sign, contact, transact or otherwise create external/legal/financial consequences. Human authorization remains mandatory unless an explicit delegated authority exists and Gauntlet verifies that delegation.

No model may escalate itself from A0-A2 into A3.

## Refinement protocol

ROUND 1: discover and qualify.
ROUND 2: create and package.
ROUND 3: attack.
ROUND 4: human relevance and monetization.
ROUND 5: accounting and audit.
ROUND 6: synthesis.
ROUND 7: Gauntlet.
ROUND 8: Truth Oracle.
ROUND 9: Elohim chooses the next highest-value reversible action.

Repeat while the packet materially improves.

Stop when:
- evidence is exhausted;
- no material improvement occurs for the configured convergence window;
- a hard blocker is reached;
- a human gate is required;
- the opportunity expires;
- or the Gauntlet quarantines the path.

## Human-attention compression

Human gates are not emitted merely because something is external.

They are batched and ranked by:
1. verified demand strength;
2. expected external value;
3. deadline;
4. fulfillment readiness;
5. reversibility;
6. authority completeness;
7. evidence quality;
8. human time required.

The system should present Biggie with the smallest useful decision surface.
