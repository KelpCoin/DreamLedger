# MARKETPLACE GAP-CLOSURE PLAN V1

## Objective

Do not attempt to clone Trade Me, Amazon or eBay feature-for-feature.

Build a differentiated Kiwi commerce loop:

FREE UTILITY -> HOUSEHOLD SAVINGS -> UNUSED-ASSET DISCOVERY -> TRUSTED LISTING PREPARATION -> BUYER-SAFE TRANSACTION -> EVIDENCE -> REPUTATION -> REPEAT USE

The free utility is the acquisition magnet.
The marketplace is the retention surface.
Trust/evidence is the moat.
Agentic commerce is the future execution layer.

## Why this is the wedge

Trade Me currently provides strong institutional trust signals, Ping, Buyer Protection and local support. In March 2026 it removed casual Marketplace success fees, made Ping the primary payment method, removed bank transfer, and increased Buyer Protection to NZ$5,000. These are direct responses to seller friction and transaction risk.

DreamLedger should therefore not compete by saying "we are safer" without operational proof.

Instead build measurable trust primitives:
- verified payment state
- seller identity/account history where legitimately available
- listing provenance
- condition evidence
- transaction evidence
- dispute trail
- fulfilment/shipping evidence
- transparent protection rules
- machine-readable proof

## Free-to-paid loop

Entry:
- free cost-of-living tools
- free seller profit calculator
- free "what can I sell?" inventory helper
- free listing quality checker
- free scam/risk checklist
- free price/profit estimate where evidence supports it

Conversion:
- save results
- create a seller account
- prepare a listing
- receive buyer-safe transaction options
- sell an unwanted item
- use proceeds to solve another household need

Retention:
- transaction history
- evidence-backed reputation
- repeat listing tools
- saved inventory
- seller analytics
- buyer protection workflow

## Do not build yet

Do not build a national general marketplace with thousands of categories.

Do not build warehouse/logistics infrastructure.

Do not promise buyer protection before the actual protection mechanism exists.

Do not manufacture seller ratings.

Do not manufacture demand.

Do not launch agent-to-agent settlement merely because the protocol exists.

Do not copy competitors' branding or protected UX.

## Build order

1. Free acquisition utility.
2. Seller inventory/listing preparation.
3. Evidence-backed trust layer.
4. One real transaction path.
5. Buyer-side discovery for that category.
6. Repeat transaction.
7. Expand categories only after transaction evidence.

## Economic test

The first marketplace milestone is not "X listings".

It is:

1 real Kiwi seller
+
1 real independent buyer
+
1 real settled transaction
+
1 fulfilled trade
+
1 evidence chain
+
1 repeatable transaction path.

Then expand.

## Competitive principle

Trade Me's current trust features are the benchmark to beat operationally, not rhetorically.

Its March 2026 changes demonstrate that trust, payments, protection and seller economics are active competitive variables.

DreamLedger's advantage should be evidence-native commerce and machine-readable transaction truth, not a generic claim of being safer.

## Confidentiality

Competitive timing thresholds, unpublished acquisition strategy, private market signals, credentials and proprietary commercial logic stay in the private sentinel store.
