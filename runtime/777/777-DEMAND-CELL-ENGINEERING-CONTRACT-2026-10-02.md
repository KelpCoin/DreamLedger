# 777 DEMAND CELL ENGINEERING CONTRACT — 2026-10-02

## Purpose

777 is DreamLedger's canonical demand-generation multiplication operator.

A 777 demand cell is:

**OFFER × AUDIENCE × MESSAGE × SURFACE × CTA → REAL HUMAN RESPONSE → SALE**

The purpose of the cell is to make the external encounter explicit, measurable and mutable.

777 is not a second ledger, commerce queue, Truth Oracle, swarm or silo framework. It is an experiment contract over the existing substrate.

## Required dimensions

### OFFER
The concrete utility, product, test, report, comparison, service or paid depth being presented.

### AUDIENCE
The externally observable population or use case for whom the proposition is intended.

### MESSAGE
The language and framing used to make the value legible.

### SURFACE
The authorized external location or channel where the audience encounters the proposition.

### CTA
The exact action requested from the human.
