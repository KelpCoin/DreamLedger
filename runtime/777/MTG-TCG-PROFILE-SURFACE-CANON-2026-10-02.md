# MTG TCG PROFILE SURFACE CANON
Date: 2026-10-02
Status: CANONICAL SURFACE DIRECTION

## Purpose

The MTG TCG silo is the first practical test of a broader DreamLedger profile model: a free, user-controlled identity that can combine community identity, collection, wants, offers and activity.

## Product idea

MySpace-style personal expression + LinkedIn-style identity/reputation + e-commerce profile.

A profile may expose, at the user's choice:
- About
- Have / collection
- Want / ISO
- Selling
- Buying
- Trading
- Formats
- Decks
- Events / activity
- City and logistics preferences
- Custom profile elements that the domain safely supports

The profile is not the marketplace itself. It is an identity and discovery surface that can contain marketplace activity.

## MTG TCG first

The initial domain is Magic: The Gathering. Use MTG TCG terminology, never CCG.

Happy Homeroid is a user/community identity inside this silo, not a DreamLedger-wide identity requirement.

## Core promise

Free to join. Free to browse. Free to build a profile. Free to list peer merchandise. 0% platform success fee on peer card sales as currently promised.

Buyers should not need an account merely to browse or buy where the current transaction flow permits it. Sellers require an account so listings are attributable.

## Design constraint

Start with the basics:
1. Find a card.
2. List a card.
3. Show what I have.
4. Show what I want.
5. Buy / sell / trade.
6. Know who I am dealing with.

Do not turn the first release into a feature encyclopedia.

## Infinite expansion model

CUBE can generate future domain/profile hypotheses and 777 can test them externally. The underlying profile primitives should remain reusable while each domain adds only the modules justified by real demand.

Potential future domains include vinyl, collectibles, cameras, books, tools, art, games and other lawful categories. These are hypotheses, not current build commitments.

## 777 relationship

A profile is a SURFACE/identity primitive. 777 remains the experimental search engine:

OFFER × AUDIENCE × MESSAGE × SURFACE × CTA → REAL HUMAN RESPONSE

Example MTG cells:
- Find local singles × Commander players × NZ availability message × MTG profile/search × Find my card
- Liquidate collection × collectors × turn unused cards into cash × profile/listing surface × List my collection
- Trade cards × active traders × find someone who wants what you have × want/have surface × Match my cards
- Play paper Magic remotely × webcam players × play tonight from home × community profile × Find an opponent

No generated profile, impression, model score or internal activity is treated as external demand.

## Anti-gatekeeping principle

Moderation protects people, transactions and the integrity of the service. It should not dictate legitimate pricing, collection style, profile expression or participation merely because an administrator prefers a particular convention.

Safety, fraud and abuse controls remain mandatory.
