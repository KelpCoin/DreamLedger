## Gauntlet service boundary

Gauntlet is a commercial surface, not merely an internal quality gate.

The service proposition is authorized adversarial stress testing. Customer-supplied systems, agents, products, workflows, documents and offers can be attacked for failure modes, contradictions, unsafe assumptions, edge cases, robustness defects and evidence weaknesses.

For security-sensitive systems, authorization and rules of engagement are mandatory. No unauthorized intrusion, credential abuse, destructive testing or uncontrolled external action is part of the service.

The commercial progression is:

FREE LIMITED TEST
-> REAL EXTERNAL RESPONSE
-> PAID DEEP TEST
-> REPORT / EVIDENCE
-> RETEST
-> RECURRING / API / USAGE

Internal Gauntlet remains the pre-exposure filter. External Gauntlet-as-a-Service is a sellable offer built on the same substrate.

## Truth-as-a-Service boundary

Truth Article / Truth-as-a-Service is a commercial verification surface.

The free tier can provide bounded claim/article/document checks. Paid tiers can provide deeper evidence collection, provenance mapping, contradiction analysis, larger inputs, structured reports, retests and API/usage access.

The service reports evidence state and uncertainty. It must not claim omniscience, legal conclusions or replacement of authoritative sources.

The Truth Oracle remains the internal economic/evidence boundary. Truth-as-a-Service is a customer-facing surface over that capability.
