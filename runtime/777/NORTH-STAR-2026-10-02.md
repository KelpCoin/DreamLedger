# DREAMLEDGER NORTH STAR - CANONICAL
Date: 2026-10-02
Status: CANONICAL

## North Star
Build a continuously operating economic substrate that discovers, stress-tests, verifies, packages, sells, fulfills, and proves small digital and physical-media economic services with minimum human attention.

The permanent asset is the substrate. Commercial surfaces may be disposable.

## Canonical commercial surfaces
1. GAUNTLET-AS-A-SERVICE
Authorized adversarial stress testing of customer-supplied systems, products, agents, workflows, offers, documents, and other testable artifacts.
Free limited testing -> paid deeper coverage, repeated/adaptive testing, richer evidence, API and continuous/retest tiers.

2. TRUTH ARTICLE / TRUTH-AS-A-SERVICE
Evidence-oriented verification of claims, articles, documents, research, and decision material.
Free limited checks -> paid deeper evidence, contradiction/opposing-evidence analysis, provenance, larger inputs, structured reports, API and recurring tiers.
Truth service reports evidence and verification state; it does not manufacture certainty or replace the authoritative Truth Oracle.

3. BILLBOARD / DOOH
Direct billboard inventory, QR commerce surfaces, measurable delivery and proof where available.

4. PROGRAMMATIC DOOH
Machine-readable inventory, automated placement workflows, measurement and delivery proof, subject to platform and inventory authorization.

5. AT-HOME ADVERTISING
Distributed residential physical-to-digital advertising surfaces with measurable placement and proof where technically and lawfully available.

6. DIGITAL ARBITRAGE - CRYPTOCURRENCY
Lawful automated research, comparison, signal extraction, decision support and market intelligence. No autonomous financial spending or trading without authorization.

7. DIGITAL ARBITRAGE - TCG
Automated pricing, comparison, inventory intelligence, deck/card analysis and opportunity detection. MTG/TCG silo separation remains intact.

## Common commercial pattern
DISCOVER -> FREE TEST -> PAY -> ENTITLEMENT/KEY -> WALL -> AUTOMATED RESULT -> PROOF -> REPEAT

The system must never promise zero-human fulfillment where human fulfillment is actually required.

## Figure Eight operating loop
CUBE -> SWARM -> ELOHIM -> GAUNTLET -> FIGURE EIGHT AUTHORITY -> FIGUREHEAD -> PAYMENT WALL -> AUTOMATED FULFILLMENT -> EVIDENCE -> TRUTH ORACLE -> LEARNING -> NEXT LOOP

Optimize VERIFIED ECONOMIC OUTCOMES per unit of HUMAN ATTENTION.

## Permanent substrate
CUBE, Swarm, Elohim, Gauntlet, Truth Oracle, Figure Eight authority, GitHub, Render, Supabase, Stripe, commerce/entitlement rails, evidence/proof infrastructure, silo boundaries, provenance and deterministic verification.

## Disposable layer
Candidate offers, product names, landing pages, pricing hypotheses, positioning, experiments, failed public surfaces and daily swarm hypotheses.

The brand is a figurehead, not the asset. Capability, distribution, verified outcomes and learning are the asset.

## Economic truth boundary
REAL DEMAND -> REAL BUYER -> AUTHORIZED ACTION -> EXTERNAL RESULT -> SETTLED PAYMENT -> FULFILLMENT -> DELIVERY/EVIDENCE -> VERIFICATION -> VERIFIED ECONOMIC OUTCOME

No internal row, checkout start, model output, API call, worker claim, CI result or entitlement is itself revenue.

## Non-negotiables
- No fabricated buyers, payments, revenue, fulfillment or proof.
- No unauthorized red-team activity.
- No autonomous financial spend without authorization.
- No customer entitlement key grants internal worker authority.
- No second economic ledger.
- No competing second Truth Oracle.
- Preserve evidence when commercial surfaces are retired.
- Cloud remains authoritative; local acceleration is optional.
