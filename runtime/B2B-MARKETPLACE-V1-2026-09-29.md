# B2B MARKETPLACE V1

Build a New Zealand-first B2B marketplace that competes through procurement depth rather than cloning consumer classifieds.

Trade Me currently supports professional sellers, business/farming/industry inventory, payments and buyer protection. In March 2026 it removed success fees for casual sellers and made Ping the primary payment option, while professional sellers remain on commercial fee arrangements. The product therefore should not depend on simply undercutting consumer marketplace fees.

V1: business identity, structured inventory, RFQs, supplier quotes, bulk pricing, multiple pricing modes, orders, payment/fulfillment/dispute states and evidence-linked verification.

Production path: CODE -> CI -> AUTH/RLS -> TEST BUSINESS -> TEST LISTING -> TEST RFQ -> TEST QUOTE -> TEST ORDER -> PAYMENT -> HUMAN REVIEW -> PRODUCTION -> REAL BUSINESSES -> VERIFIED TRANSACTIONS.

No live inventory, payments, buyer accounts or revenue are claimed by this artifact.
