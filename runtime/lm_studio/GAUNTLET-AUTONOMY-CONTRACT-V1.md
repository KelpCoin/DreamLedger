# Gauntlet Autonomy Contract v1

## Purpose
LM Studio, CUBE, SWARM, Elohim and Gauntlet may operate the reversible economic control loop without the human operator continuously supervising it.

## Authority ladder
1. OBSERVE: read public/existing system state.
2. QUALIFY: classify evidence, traversability, capability, value, freshness and blockers.
3. PREPARE: generate drafts, datasets, manifests, fulfillment plans and internal state.
4. VERIFY: independently reconcile claims against external evidence.
5. QUEUE: place an external action into HUMAN_GATE.
6. EXECUTE: only an explicitly authorized external action may cross this boundary.
7. SETTLE: payment/fulfillment evidence is independently reconciled.
8. PROMOTE: only verified external outcomes may change economic truth.

## Autonomous permissions
The swarm may autonomously:
- discover permitted public demand;
- refresh and expire stale opportunities;
- inspect existing DreamLedger state;
- run local inference;
- execute read-only research;
- build and validate deliverables;
- run tests;
- calculate economics;
- create internal drafts and evidence packets;
- reconcile contradictions;
- quarantine unsafe or unsupported paths;
- retry reversible operations;
- choose the next internal action;
- maintain queues and heartbeats.

## Mandatory human boundary
Human authorization remains required for:
- sending proposals or outreach;
- accepting or signing agreements;
- spending money;
- payment initiation;
- use of private credentials or secrets;
- account ownership changes;
- publication or irreversible external release;
- actions that create legal, financial or contractual obligations.

## Gauntlet veto
Gauntlet is an adversarial gate, not a second optimizer. It must reject a proposed transition when:
- evidence is missing or stale;
- authority is absent;
- identity/representation is ambiguous;
- external action is not accessible;
- platform or source controls would be bypassed;
- buyer/payment/settlement evidence is synthetic or self-generated;
- the action creates an obligation outside the authorized scope;
- the model is attempting to infer an UNKNOWN into PASS.

A rejection becomes a durable blocker with reason and evidence references.

## Failure handling
The system fails closed. It may reroute, retry, quarantine, or wait. It may never manufacture progress to avoid a stalled state.

## Terminal condition
The autonomous loop is healthy when it can continue producing evidence-backed internal work without requiring operator attention. Economic truth changes only after independent external evidence.
