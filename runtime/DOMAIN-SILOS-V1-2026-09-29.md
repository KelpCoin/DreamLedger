# DOMAIN SILOS V1

These are independent demand and capability silos. They share common discovery, provenance, qualification, offer, fulfillment and evidence machinery, but each has its own catalog and evidence state.

## 1. MUSIC_MEDIA
Scope:
- recorded music
- vinyl, CDs, cassettes and other physical formats
- music/media collectibles
- artist and release research
- media-format knowledge products
- lawful media-related services

Demand signals:
- NZ music consumption and sales
- physical-format demand
- marketplace listings and wanted signals
- artist/release-specific searches

Current evidence:
NZ On Air reports 37% of New Zealanders actively look for NZ music and 41% would listen to more NZ music if surfaced by their streaming service. Recorded Music NZ reports 2025 New Zealand music streaming approaching 900 million streams and physical album sales up 13% year-on-year.

Guardrails:
- no unauthorized copying or distribution
- no copyrighted media repackaging without rights
- marketplace rules remain source-specific

## 2. RETRO_GAMES
Scope:
- retro consoles
- cartridges/discs
- accessories
- manuals and boxed items
- pricing/valuation research
- collection identification
- restoration and cataloguing knowledge products

Demand signals:
- secondhand marketplace activity
- wanted listings
- price history
- collection-specific requests

Guardrails:
- distinguish physical original games from copied digital content
- no ROM distribution
- no account/subscription resale where prohibited
- source-policy gate before automated collection

## 3. BESPOKE_LEARNING
Scope:
- one-person learning paths
- visual/practical learning
- skill-specific curricula
- diagnostic learning plans
- project-based progression
- resource curation
- progress/evidence tracking

Operating primitive:
PERSON -> GOAL -> CURRENT LEVEL -> CONSTRAINTS -> CURRICULUM -> PRACTICE -> FEEDBACK -> EVIDENCE -> NEXT STEP

This is a learning silo, not a generic content factory. Personal/private learning can remain isolated from public commercial silos.

## 4. COURSES
Scope:
- structured short courses
- practical mini-courses
- guided cohorts or self-paced material
- assessments and exercises
- templates/workbooks
- completion evidence
- niche professional and hobby education

Commercial primitive:
DEMAND -> COURSE PROMISE -> SAMPLE -> PAID OFFER -> DELIVERY -> COMPLETION -> OUTCOME -> REPEAT/REFERRAL

Guardrail:
A course is not validated merely because it exists. Promotion requires external demand and then real buyer/fulfillment evidence.

## 5. HORSE_RACING
Scope:
- race data research
- historical results
- form/feature analysis
- prediction research
- backtesting
- paper-trading evidence

State:
RESEARCH -> BACKTEST -> OUT_OF_SAMPLE -> PAPER -> VERIFIED_EDGE -> HUMAN_GATE -> LIVE

No wagering automation. No prediction treated as revenue.

## Shared silo contract

Every silo must retain:
- source provenance
- observed timestamp
- demand evidence
- qualification state
- capability state
- access/traversability state
- offer state
- transaction state
- fulfillment state
- verified outcome state

No internal activity counts as economic success.

Promotion path:
OBSERVED -> QUALIFIED -> TRAVERSABLE -> CAPABLE -> OFFERABLE -> HUMAN_GATE -> TRANSACTED -> FULFILLED -> VERIFIED

The silos are intentionally parallel. No category receives a fabricated revenue status because another silo succeeds.
