# DREAMLEDGER UNIVERSAL HEADER ECONOMIC LAWS V1

Date: 2026-09-29

The public header is a permanent economic/navigation layer, not decoration.

Permanent primitives:

1. TRUTH ORACLE
Primary trust primitive. Public promise: help people save money and make decisions with evidence. Missing evidence stays UNKNOWN. Future monetization can include paid evidence-backed audits, decision reports, commercial truth reviews and transaction verification.

2. AVATAR
Persistent identity and attachment primitive. Route: /avatar. Future monetization can include cosmetic upgrades, identity continuity, world-specific items and utility-bearing game assets. Do not claim item utility or pay-to-win mechanics until implemented.

3. ACCOUNT
Log in and Create account stay visible. Identity continuity turns anonymous utility into a persistent relationship and transaction history. An account is not a buyer until an attributable external transaction settles.

4. FIND MONEY
Persistent acquisition and economic utility. Route: /free. This is the universal return reason: inspect what can be saved, recovered or sold before spending more.

5. BILLBOARD
Persistent attention inventory. Route: /billboard. Programmatic DOOH can become an advertising rail. Only paid, reviewed and published reservations count as commercial outcomes.

6. GLOBAL TICKER
One universal stream. It may eventually carry verified public facts, source-backed market signals and platform updates. Never fabricate news, activity or market claims. Potential future monetization includes sponsored placement, paid distribution and market intelligence.

7. CONTEXT TICKER
A second ticker changes with the current niche or world. MTG, avatars, billboard, cost-of-living tools and future worlds can each surface relevant verified signals without changing the global shell.

8. WORLD/CONTEXT
The header should always tell the visitor where they are and provide a route back to that economy. This creates persistent cross-world navigation without exposing internal architecture.

The missing permanent primitive before this build was FIND MONEY. The header therefore makes five things continuously visible:

TRUTH · IDENTITY · MONEY · ATTENTION · ACCOUNT

Everything else can rotate underneath them.

Truth boundary:

Header impressions, clicks, avatar creation, accounts, ticker views and billboard drafts are not revenue. Verified economic truth still requires an independent external buyer, attributable settled payment, fulfillment and evidence.

Implementation:
- public/site-header.js supplies the universal shell.
- public/server.js injects it into served HTML so existing and future static pages receive the header without duplicated navigation markup.
- /truth-oracle is mapped to the existing Truth Oracle page.
- Public-facing header language excludes internal architecture names.
