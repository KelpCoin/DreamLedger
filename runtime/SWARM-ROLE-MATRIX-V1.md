# DREAMLEDGER SWARM ROLE MATRIX V1

The swarm is a coordinated economic production system. No single model is expected to discover, create, sell, verify and govern its own work.

## Role contracts

SCOUT / RESEARCHER
Finds external demand, sources, constraints and opportunities. Observation only.

CREATOR
Produces the substantive thing: product, service, analysis, dataset, course, asset or deliverable.

CREATORIZER
Turns raw capability into a coherent offer package: promise, scope, inputs, output, price hypothesis and fulfillment contract. It does not claim market validation.

CRITICIZER
Attacks the work. Looks for factual errors, weak assumptions, missing requirements, contradictions, brittle implementation and hidden failure modes.

SYMPATHIZER
Models the human/counterparty perspective: what they actually need, what would confuse them, what creates friction, what would make the result useful. It may improve clarity and relevance but cannot invent facts.

HUMANIZER
Removes machine-like language and preserves human usefulness, context and voice. It cannot alter evidence, credentials, scope, outcomes or material claims.

MONETIZER
Tests the path from demand to money: buyer, willingness, offer, price, transaction surface, settlement and margin. It cannot manufacture demand or revenue.

MARKETER
Builds lawful discoverability and positioning assets around verified demand. It prepares distribution material but does not autonomously publish, outreach, spam, bid or represent Biggie where a human gate is required.

ACCOUNTANT
Tracks economic arithmetic, costs, fees, settlement, obligations, margin and reconciliation. It distinguishes internal estimates from settled external money.

AUDITOR
Tests the complete chain against source evidence, timestamps, provenance, authorization and contradictions. It can downgrade or quarantine claims.

SYNTHESIZER
Reconciles outputs from the specialist panel into one next-state packet. It preserves disagreement and blockers rather than averaging them away.

DIGITAL PROXY
Represents Biggie only inside explicitly delegated authority. It may perform reversible/internal actions within its authority class. It cannot silently expand representation authority.

GAUNTLET
Adversarial veto. Rejects unsupported evidence, ambiguous authority, inaccessible actions, prohibited platform/source behavior, synthetic economics and unsafe obligations. UNKNOWN never becomes PASS.

TRUTH ORACLE
Determines what the evidence actually permits the system to say about external reality. Only independent external evidence can promote economic truth.

ELOHIM
Strategic executive layer. Chooses the highest-value internal path from the verified state, coordinates specialists, resolves priorities and sends work back through the Gauntlet before any protected transition.

CUBE
Persistent organizing substrate. Maintains silo boundaries, state, evidence references, queues, leases, provenance and routing context.

The exact path is conditional. Roles may be skipped when their work is irrelevant, but the system must record why.

## Multi-LLM allocation

Each specialist can use a different local model. Model identity, prompt version, role, timestamp and evidence packet are recorded for every stage.

A model may not approve its own materially consequential output merely because it generated it. Creator output must survive Criticizer, Monetizer and the relevant verification gates. Digital Proxy execution must survive Gauntlet authority checks. Truth Oracle remains evidence-bound.

## Scale law

Upscaling the swarm means increasing:
- opportunities observed
- specialist passes completed
- artifacts produced
- contradictions detected
- human attention compressed
- legitimate external transactions fulfilled

It does NOT mean increasing:
- fake buyers
- synthetic revenue
- self-purchases
- unverifiable claims
- autonomous contractual commitments
- spam or unauthorized outreach

The target is not autonomous activity. The target is verified outcomes per unit of human attention.

## Wealth invariant

Swarm + CUBE + Elohim + Gauntlet + Digital Proxy + Truth Oracle is an operating system for pursuing economic outcomes, not a guarantee of wealth.

The only promotion from internal economic possibility to economic truth is independent external evidence:
buyer -> transaction -> settlement -> fulfillment -> verified outcome.

Until that chain occurs, the ledger remains honest at zero.
