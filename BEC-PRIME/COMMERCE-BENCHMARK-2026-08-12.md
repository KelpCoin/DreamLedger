# Commerce benchmark and synthesis

## Scope

The target is not to clone one platform. The target is to take the strongest commercially useful primitives from adjacent platforms and compile them into a governed, silo-aware, thumb-first commerce surface.

## Ten closest reference systems

1. Shopify: collections, product discovery, recommendations and merchant-grade commerce primitives.
2. Amazon: horizontal discovery, personalization, intent-aware recommendations and return-loop shopping.
3. eBay: timed auctions and ending-soon urgency.
4. Etsy: marketplace discovery and category-driven browsing.
5. Lemon Squeezy: digital-product storefronts, variants, checkout and automated delivery.
6. Fourthwall: products plus memberships, digital delivery, discounts and community integrations.
7. Ko-fi: low-friction creator shop, commissions, memberships and lightweight monetization.
8. Gumroad: simple digital commerce, memberships, subscriptions, discoverability and immediate delivery.
9. BigCommerce: channel abstraction and multi-surface commerce distribution.
10. Stripe Agentic Commerce: structured catalog, machine-readable commerce and agent-mediated checkout/payment primitives.

## Synthesis

Take from Shopify: collection semantics and recommendation slots.
Take from Amazon: horizontal discovery, personalization and intent-aware return loops.
Take from eBay: three distinct auction clocks and ending-soon surfaces.
Take from Etsy: marketplace-style discovery and community inventory.
Take from Lemon Squeezy: evergreen digital fulfilment and entitlement-friendly product variants.
Take from Fourthwall: product-to-membership progression and gated perks.
Take from Ko-fi: low-friction purchase and community monetization.
Take from Gumroad: immediate digital delivery and simple product publishing.
Take from BigCommerce: channel-aware product distribution.
Take from Stripe: structured product data, machine-readable feeds and agentic commerce readiness.

## DreamLedger differentiators

- Public front door -> silo -> second carousel.
- Thumb-first horizontal rails rather than desktop-first product grids.
- Server-authoritative product state.
- Payment truth comes from signed Stripe events, not browser redirects.
- Entitlement is a separate server-side primitive.
- Permanent and temporary entitlements.
- Protected goods outside the public website root.
- 100 governed candidates split 50 human-first / 50 agent-ready.
- Gauntlet Net activation gate.
- Elohim evidence/contradiction/escalation boundary.
- Stable QR doorway.
- Privacy-preserving local timezone/language detection and optional geolocation permission.
- NZ-first HappyHomarid MTG silo kept mechanically separate.
- Cryptocurrency payments disabled; Kelp Coin can later be a loyalty/cosmetics layer rather than a payment rail.

## Commercial rule

"100 products" means 100 opportunities compiled into the catalog. It does not mean 100 products are falsely presented as purchasable. Activation requires real evidence, real fulfilment, approval and a valid payment path.
