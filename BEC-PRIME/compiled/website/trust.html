Trust centre

How DreamLedger treats money, listings, auctions, and evidence. Designed so a careful operator, auditor, or buyer can verify claims without theatre.

Fail closed · no invented revenue

Economic truth

  • Revenue = independently evidenced external payment + correct attribution + fulfillment + proof.
  • Checkout starts, abandoned carts, payment links, database rows, tests, simulations, and generated assets are not revenue.
  • Public CTA cards only appear for cells that cleared a concrete offer, purchase path, and required evidence.

Fees (current public offers)

  • 0% DreamLedger success fee on current public offers.
  • Stripe (or processor) fees still apply to card payments.
  • Listing intake is free; publication requires approval.

Silo boundaries

Identity

Dream Ledger / avatar surfaces do not mix commerce inventory.

MTG

Collectibles and diagnostics stay in the MTG commercial boundary.

Billboard

Finite placements; payment does not equal automatic publication.

B2B / Marketplace

Seller intake is PENDING_APPROVAL until reviewed.

Auctions

  • Minimum increment enforced server-side.
  • Anti-sniping extension on late bids.
  • Reserve supported; public bidder identifiers are hashed.
  • Bid proof artifacts are written for auditability.
  • Approval and checkout gates: bidding/settlement only when the lot is cleared.
  • A bid is not settlement. Settlement still requires verified payment.

News ticker

  • Editorial items are source-attributed and silo-scoped where applicable.
  • Ticker is informational; it does not invent sales volume or social proof.

What we will not do

  • Show fake live buyers or fabricated bid activity.
  • Count internal or simulated activity as revenue.
  • Publish unapproved listings or mix restricted silos into the public market.
