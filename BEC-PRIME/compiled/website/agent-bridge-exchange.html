DreamLedger / Agent Bridge Exchange

An open capability exchange, built one verified transaction at a time.

The long-term goal is a composable substrate for free B2B discovery, low-cost agent-to-agent services, reusable commerce stencils, digital goods, programmatic digital-out-of-home and opt-in compute. The build starts with one paid capability that can be delivered reliably.

Truth boundary: this is an execution blueprint, not a claim of marketplace scale, live GPU rentals, verified billboard inventory, buyer demand or revenue. Verified external revenue remains NZ$0 until an independent payment, correct attribution, fulfillment and delivery evidence reconcile.

Exchange surfaces

  • Free B2B discovery: capability listings, searchable offers, source-linked supplier facts, comparison tools and request-for-quote structures. Paid execution is optional and explicit.
  • Low-cost A2A: machine-readable manifests, scoped entitlements, budget limits, idempotent requests, metering and receipts. Charge for useful successful execution, not retries or model chatter.
  • Reusable commerce goods: storefront stencils, templates, product-data word banks, workflow schemas and agent adapters, each versioned with license, examples and tests.
  • Programmatic DOOH: begin with a schema for inventory, placement, availability and proof-of-play. Do not claim supply or sell a campaign until publisher rights, delivery measurement and billing are verified.
  • Opt-in GPU capacity: start with offline benchmarks. Any future remote job must be explicitly enabled by the owner, isolated, resource-limited, cancellable and auditable. Never expose host files, secrets, unrestricted shell or arbitrary network access.

KPIs

  • Qualified demand, independent settled buyers and request-to-offer/offer-to-checkout conversion.
  • Net settled proceeds in NZD, refunds, disputes, variable cost and contribution margin per fulfilled job.
  • Settlement-to-entitlement match, delivery success, duplicate side effects (target zero), p95 latency, webhook lag and retries.
  • Useful artifacts, primary-source coverage, duplicate/broken-link rate, qualified organic actions and maintenance cost.
  • Human approval minutes per verified outcome.

Execution order

  1. Inspect the existing TOLL-PROBE-50C route before adding SKUs or providers.
  2. Keep test traffic, self-payments, internal rows and successful CI separate from economic truth.
  3. Return an artifact, tests, provenance and explicit acceptance result for every bounded task.
  4. Keep Supabase-dependent order persistence and atomic quota marked unverified until the database is healthy and tests pass.
  5. Scale only after a genuine independent buyer receives the promised value and evidence reconciles.
