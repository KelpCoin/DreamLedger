Privacy notice

Published 11 October 2026. This is a public baseline notice and should be reviewed against the services and data flows before being treated as a complete legal policy.

Information you provide

Information you choose to submit to a DreamLedger service may be processed to provide that service, respond to a request, protect the site, or maintain records needed for the transaction. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, health information, or other sensitive information unless a service explicitly requires it and provides a suitable private channel.

Browser-only tools

The supplier quote comparison worksheet is designed to process entered comparison data in your browser. Its worksheet inputs are not uploaded by that tool. Exporting a file is an action you control.

Payments and third parties

Where checkout is offered, Stripe processes payment information through its own service. DreamLedger does not ask you to send card details in a message. Hosting, security, and service providers may process technical request information to operate and protect the site. Their own privacy terms may also apply.

Public support channels

If you contact us through a public issue tracker, your message may be visible to anyone. Do not include private or sensitive details there. A private support channel is not represented as available unless explicitly provided.

Retention and requests

Retention depends on the service, security needs, and transaction records. To ask a privacy question or request review of information, use the contact route linked below and describe the request without including sensitive information in a public post.

Updates

This notice may be updated as services and data practices change. It is not a substitute for a reviewed privacy policy tailored to each applicable jurisdiction.
