Policies

Plain summary for buyers and sellers. Not a substitute for full legal terms where those exist.

Fees

  • Platform success fee on current public offers: 0%. DreamLedger does not take a percentage of the sale price on those offers.
  • Stripe (or other processor) fees still apply to card payments.
  • Listing intake is free. Publication requires approval.
  • Fees may change for future products; changes will be stated on the offer.

Listings

  • Submitted listings enter PENDING_APPROVAL. Nothing is public until approved.
  • Illegal, fraudulent, hateful, or otherwise prohibited content will not be published.
  • Sellers must be entitled to sell what they list and must describe condition and delivery accurately.
  • Billboard placements require human review before going live on the canvas.

Payments & revenue truth

  • Checkout is handled by Stripe. Card details are not stored on DreamLedger.
  • A started checkout is not a sale. Abandoned carts are not revenue.
  • Verified revenue requires an independent external payment, correct attribution, and fulfillment evidence.

Fulfillment

  • Digital products: delivery method is stated on the offer (often instant or short SLA after payment).
  • Physical goods: seller or operator fulfills; shipping terms should be stated at listing.
  • Billboard: payment does not equal automatic publication — review gate applies.

Privacy (summary)

  • Contact details collected for checkout, fulfillment, or seller intake are used for those purposes.
  • We do not sell personal data as a product.
  • Processor (Stripe) has its own privacy policy for payment data.

Contact

For listing or payment issues, use the email you provided at intake or checkout, or the contact path on your account surface when logged in.
