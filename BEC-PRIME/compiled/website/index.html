Trust boundary

Trust is a product feature.

DreamLedger does not treat checkout starts, internal database rows, simulations or generated artifacts as revenue. Economic status is promoted only when the external evidence supports it.

  • No customer payment grants private-system access.
  • Service access is scoped to the purchased entitlement.
  • Keys can carry expiry, quota and road identity.
  • Internal control-plane terminology is kept separate from public service responses.
  • Public distribution remains authorization-gated.
