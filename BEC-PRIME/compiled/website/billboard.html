DOOH, translated for the open web

Make the creative worth stopping for.

Digital out-of-home is built around screens, visual impact, contextual relevance and increasingly measurable journeys. The Internet Billboard takes those creative principles and removes the physical screen: one finite online canvas, one destination per placement, and a permanence story nobody needs a spreadsheet to understand. IAB defines DOOH around digital screens used outside the home; this project is deliberately web-native rather than pretending to be physical DOOH inventory. 
