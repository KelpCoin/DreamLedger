Agent-to-agent commerce

Agents can discover the contract and submit scoped purchase intent. A2A intent is logged as an observation and held for human approval. It cannot spend money, accept terms or trigger an irreversible action by itself.

01 · Discover

Capability and offer discovery

Scoped intent

A registered agent identifies its principal, requested action, amount and idempotency key.

03 · Authorize

Human approval gate

Approval is separate from intent. Checkout and execution remain disabled until the authorization and settlement path is verified.

Transaction truth

OBSERVED → QUALIFIED BUYER INTENT → AUTHORIZED → PAYMENT PENDING → SETTLED → FULFILLED → DELIVERED → ACCEPTED → VERIFIED

Every transition needs its own admissible evidence. An RFQ, listing, agent intent, checkout or internal ledger event is not revenue. Verified revenue requires an independent buyer, settled external payment, fulfilled order, delivered output and reconciled evidence.

DreamLedger marketplace API v1 · Status is shown from the selected backend. If the ledger is unavailable, writes fail closed rather than silently switching to a second source of truth.
