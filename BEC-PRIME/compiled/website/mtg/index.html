Know what you’re getting

What does “free” cover?

Browsing, the deck workbench and preparing peer trade posts are free. DreamLedger charges no peer listing fee or trade commission. Card prices, postage and any payment-provider charges are agreed separately.

How do I agree on condition?

Ask for front and back photos of the actual card, with close-ups of wear. Name the set, collector number, language and finish. Agree on Near Mint, Lightly Played, Moderately Played, Heavily Played or Damaged in writing; the label alone is not a substitute for photos.

How do I arrange a trade?

Agree on both card lists, exact printings, quantities, condition, any cash difference and the handover method. Keep a copy of the agreement. Confirm tracked-post costs or a public meetup location before either person sends cards or money.

Can I buy a trust badge?

No. Memberships, donations and purchases do not establish a completed trade or increase trade reputation. There are no verified trade scores on this site yet.
