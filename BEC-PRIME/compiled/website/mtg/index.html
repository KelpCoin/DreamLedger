How buying works

Simple.

Pick an item, pay, then the economic ledger records the settlement when Stripe confirms the payment webhook.

1. Select a listing. The listing has an ID, price and inventory count.

2. Click BUY NOW. The button opens the configured Stripe payment surface.

3. Payment settles. Checkout completion is not counted as revenue until the signed webhook is received and the durable ledger records it.

4. Physical fulfilment. The sold item is reserved against the inventory record and fulfilment follows the transaction proof.
