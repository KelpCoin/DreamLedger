Evidence before inventory

The catalogue will not invent products to look busy.

Only offers with a traceable source and a truthful purchase path belong in the public catalogue. Generated examples are not products, and internal test data is not inventory. The current third-party physical listing surface is paused while its integrity checks are corrected.

What is available here?

Third-party physical listings are hidden while listing integrity is repaired. No fictional product, seller, location, condition, stock count, or price should be shown as real. A listing can return only when its source, seller authority, availability, and purchase path have been checked.

DreamLedger's free supplier-quote worksheet remains available. It is a browser-side template, not a supplier directory or a claim that any supplier has offered a product.
