# North Star — BEC / DreamLedger

**For every LLM and operator touching this system.**

Build a **self-looking-after commercial system** that produces durable verified external revenue with **minimal ongoing human intervention**, after 15+ months of unpaid construction.

Success is not green CI, pretty surfaces, or internal claims.  
Success is **repeated live external settlement → fulfilment → proof**, at increasing loop count.

## What must never be faked

- Revenue without live external payment evidence  
- Inventory that does not exist  
- Cross-silo contamination of proof

## Operator load target

Routine discovery, settlement recognition, fulfilment triggers, and face compilation trend toward **system + agents**.
