# MONEY-03 100-PROSPECT PLAYBOOK

Objective: obtain the first verified NZD 49 payment, then repeat the same sale until conversion evidence exists.

## WHY THIS MARKET

Shopify says AI-referred orders grew nearly 13x year over year in Q1 2026 and says merchants should improve product data and AI discoverability. Shopify also says millions of merchants can sell into major AI channels through Agentic Storefronts. This makes merchant-side data quality and transaction readiness a commercially legible problem.

Do not sell "AI magic." Sell diagnosis of an observable commerce surface.

## PROSPECT POOL

Build 100 prospects from public business sources. Start with NZ Shopify stores, then expand to WooCommerce and other commerce stacks.

Useful discovery pools include:
- public NZ Shopify-store directories
- Google search results for NZ product categories
- NZ business directories
- public company websites
- public LinkedIn/company pages where outreach is permitted

Do not scrape private data. Do not buy or upload personal data into the repository. Record only business-level information needed to qualify the sale.

## PRIORITY VERTICALS

1. Electronics and computers
2. Health and wellness products
3. Home and garden
4. Sports and outdoor
5. Fashion and apparel
6. Beauty and personal care
7. Baby and kids
8. Hobbies and crafts
9. Automotive
10. B2B/wholesale

Reason: richer catalogs and higher attribute complexity make machine interpretation more commercially consequential.

## 10-POINT QUALIFIER

Give 0-2 points each:

- Catalog complexity
- Purchase value / commercial consequence
- Public data completeness gap
- Machine-readable structure gap
- Checkout/actionability gap
- Trust/policy gap
- AI discovery relevance
- Owner accessibility
- Visible growth/marketing activity
- Clear remediation opportunity

Prioritize 14+ out of 20.

## 60-SECOND AUDIT

For each prospect inspect:

1. Homepage
2. One category page
3. Three representative product/service pages
4. Search/filter behavior
5. Price, variant and availability information
6. Structured data where publicly observable
7. Returns/shipping/contact/trust information
8. Checkout path

Capture 1-3 concrete defects. Stop there. The $49 audit is the product, not the free audit.

## OUTREACH

Use one specific observation.

"I checked [BUSINESS]. One thing I noticed: [OBSERVABLE GAP]. That can make it harder for machine shopping systems to interpret or act on the offer. I'm running a NZD $49 commerce-readiness audit that checks discoverability, product data, trust and transaction path. Want the link?"

Do not claim that the defect definitely causes lost sales. Say it can create friction or reduce machine interpretability.

## FOLLOW-UP

Follow-up 1: 48 hours later, one sentence and the same link.

Follow-up 2: 4-7 days later, offer to send one example finding from the public site.

Stop after reasonable attempts. Respect platform rules, opt-outs and anti-spam requirements.

## MEASUREMENT

Track:
- prospects qualified
- outreach sent
- replies
- positive replies
- checkout visits
- payments
- verified transaction IDs
- audits delivered
- upsells

Primary metric: verified payments / qualified prospects.

Secondary metric: qualified prospects / audited prospects.

## MONEY GATES

0 payments: do not build new features.
1 payment: deliver, obtain permission for proof, refine offer.
3 payments: test NZD 149 remediation plan.
10 payments: productize recurring audit workflow.

Revenue is zero until settlement evidence exists.
