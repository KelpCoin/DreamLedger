# MONEY-04 Audit Specification

The $49 product must produce an artifact that a merchant can understand in one sitting and act on immediately.

## OUTPUT

One concise readiness report with:

1. Executive verdict: READY / PARTIAL / BLOCKED
2. Evidence table: URL, observed fact, consequence
3. Machine readability findings
4. Product/service interpretation findings
5. Actionability findings
6. Trust and decision-signal findings
7. Priority fixes ranked P0/P1/P2
8. Five-minute verification steps
9. Optional remediation estimate

## TESTS

A. Can a machine identify the merchant, offer, product/service, price and availability?
B. Can it distinguish variants/options and constraints?
C. Can it determine what is currently purchasable?
D. Can it locate shipping, returns, cancellation and contact information where relevant?
E. Can it reach a stable purchase or enquiry action?
F. Are important claims supported by first-party evidence?
G. Are structured data, visible content and transactional facts consistent?

## EVIDENCE STANDARD

Every failure needs a URL or reproducible observation. Screenshots may be added by the operator, but the report must remain understandable without them.

Do not claim that a failure guarantees lost sales. State the mechanism and uncertainty.

## COMMERCIAL CONVERSION

If the audit finds high-impact gaps, offer a fixed remediation package rather than immediately selling open-ended consulting.

NZD 149: prioritized remediation blueprint.
NZD 499: implementation starter.
NZD 999+: broader implementation only after scope is understood.

## RESEARCH SIGNAL

Recent 2026 research frames agent-ready websites around interpretability, executability and decision reliability. A controlled study reported substantially higher browser-agent task success for an agent-ready version than a human-oriented baseline. This supports testing those dimensions, not claiming guaranteed commercial lift.
