# MONEY-09 First Customer Attack

Objective: obtain the first verified NZD 49 payment without adding product features.

## MARKET SIGNAL

Shopify says millions of merchants can now sell through AI conversations, including ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Mode and Gemini. This makes merchant-side product data and actionability a live commercial concern, not a speculative future feature.

## TARGET

Start with merchants where an agent has more work to do than a human:

- high-value products
- many variants
- technical specifications
- delivery constraints
- stock constraints
- qualification-heavy services

Prefer NZ merchants with public ecommerce surfaces and an identifiable owner or commercial contact.

## FAST TEST

For each prospect:

1. Pick the hardest product/service.
2. Ask three deterministic questions using only public merchant evidence.
3. Record UNDERSTAND, DECIDE and ACT results.
4. Capture one screenshot or URL per material finding.
5. Write a two-sentence personalized finding.
6. Prepare the NZD 49 offer.

## OUTREACH

No automated public posting.
No unsolicited bulk messaging.
Use only channels where outreach is permitted and personally approve the send.

Suggested message structure:

"I checked [specific product/service] on [merchant]. I found [specific observable issue]. That can make machine-assisted comparison/action less reliable. I run a NZD 49 evidence-backed commerce surface audit that tests whether an AI agent can understand, decide and reach the correct commercial action. Want me to run the full check?"

## STOP CONDITIONS

Stop prospecting that merchant when:

- the evidence is weak;
- the issue is not commercially meaningful;
- the business is clearly not a fit;
- contact would violate channel rules;
- the merchant declines.

Move to the next qualified merchant.
