# MONEY-05 Offer Ladder

The first sale is not the business. It is the proof that the acquisition wedge works.

## LADDER

ENTRY
NZD 49 Agentic Commerce Readiness Audit

Deliverable: evidence-backed diagnosis, prioritized fixes, verification steps.

NEXT
NZD 149 Remediation Blueprint

Deliverable: implementation-ready change list, acceptance criteria, page/data priorities, and verification plan.

CORE
NZD 499 Implementation Starter

Deliverable: bounded implementation of the highest-value fixes on one commerce surface.

EXPANSION
NZD 999+ Commerce Readiness Build

Only quote after scope, access requirements, platform constraints and acceptance criteria are known.

## CONVERSION RULES

Do not upsell a customer before the $49 deliverable is complete.
Do not promise a traffic or revenue increase without measured evidence.
Do not bundle unrelated services into the first purchase.
Use the customer's actual findings to determine the next product.

## ECONOMIC MODEL

100 qualified prospects
20 meaningful conversations
5 paid or high-intent opportunities
1 first sale

After proof:
repeat the acquisition loop, measure conversion at every stage, and raise price only when evidence supports a larger outcome.

## CAPITAL RULE

No paid ads until organic/direct acquisition demonstrates a positive conversion signal.
No new software purchase unless it removes a demonstrated bottleneck.
No additional product SKU until the current SKU has generated real customer evidence.
