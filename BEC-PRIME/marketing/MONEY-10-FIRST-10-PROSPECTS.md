# MONEY-10 FIRST 10 PROSPECTS

Objective: create ten evidence-backed, approval-ready offers. No fake findings. No automatic outreach.

## TARGET ORDER

1. PC Traders NZ: 773 products; directory-listed average price NZD 1,222.64. High-value catalog makes complex product interpretation commercially meaningful.
2. Allied Medical: 1,037 products; directory-listed average price NZD 1,061.20. High-value products plus healthcare constraints justify careful evidence testing.
3. Large NZ home/furniture merchant: prioritize complex dimensions, variants and delivery constraints.
4. Large NZ electronics merchant: prioritize spec-heavy comparison and configurable products.
5. Large NZ sports merchant: prioritize fit, size, technical attributes and inventory.
6. Large NZ outdoor merchant: prioritize compatibility, specifications and fulfilment.
7. Large NZ fashion merchant: prioritize variant availability and returns/fit information.
8. Large NZ beauty merchant: prioritize ingredients, usage constraints and product relationships.
9. Large NZ food merchant: prioritize pack sizes, dietary attributes and delivery.
10. Large NZ B2B/industrial merchant: prioritize technical specifications, compatibility and enquiry action.

## TEST

For each prospect choose one high-value or high-complexity SKU/service.

UNDERSTAND: can the key identity, price, options and constraints be extracted correctly?
DECIDE: can a stated buyer requirement be answered from merchant evidence without guessing?
ACT: can the intended purchase/enquiry path be reached unambiguously?

## EVIDENCE RECORD

Capture URL, timestamp, observed fact, expected fact, result, screenshot/reference and commercial implication.

Do not infer a defect from a category label. Verify it directly.

## OFFER

If a meaningful gap is verified:

"I tested one of your more complex offers against the way AI shopping systems need to understand, compare and act on products. I found [specific evidence]. I can run the full evidence-backed commerce readiness audit for NZD 49 and give you the prioritized fixes."

## TRUTH GATE

No revenue claim without a real payment settlement and transaction_id.
No customer claim without a real customer.
No public outreach without explicit approval.

## MARKET BASIS

Shopify states that Agentic Storefronts let merchants sell through AI conversations and that Shopify Catalog structures product data for AI discovery. Shopify also describes UCP as the open standard for agent transactions. The NZ store universe is large enough for systematic prospecting: StoreCensus reports 14,689 NZ Shopify stores updated July 27, 2026, while EachSpy reports 17,601 active stores updated July 25, 2026.
