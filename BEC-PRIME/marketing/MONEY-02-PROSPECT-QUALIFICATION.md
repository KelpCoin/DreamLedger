# MONEY-02 Prospect Qualification

Purpose: turn the NZD $49 Agentic Commerce Readiness offer into a repeatable prospecting queue.

## BUYER PROFILE

Prioritize businesses that already sell online and have a material commercial reason to be machine-readable.

Score each prospect 0-2 on every factor:

- Online checkout exists
- Public catalog or service menu exists
- Public prices or clear buying path exists
- Multiple products/services create comparison complexity
- High-value purchase or meaningful repeat purchase
- Owner/operator is directly reachable
- Weak structured product/service information is visible
- Weak trust/policy information is visible
- AI discoverability is commercially relevant
- Fixes could plausibly increase conversion or discovery

TARGET SCORE: 14+

## AUDIT-FIRST EVIDENCE

Before outreach, capture only observable facts:

1. URL
2. Offer/product name
3. Price or buying path
4. Product/service data quality gap
5. Discoverability gap
6. Checkout/actionability gap
7. Trust/policy gap
8. One sentence explaining why the gap matters commercially

Never invent a score, benchmark, AI traffic claim, customer result, or protocol certification.

## CONVERSION RULE

Do not lead with protocols. Lead with the observed business gap.

Bad: "You need UCP/ACP/AP2."
Good: "Your product pages expose price and images, but variants and availability are inconsistent, which makes machine comparison unreliable. I can audit the commerce surface for NZD $49."

NZD 499+: implementation

Only advance a prospect after a real paid engagement and documented proof.
