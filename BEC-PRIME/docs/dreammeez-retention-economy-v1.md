# DreamMeez Retention Economy v1

DreamMeez uses deterministic streak milestones to reward repeat engagement without manufacturing revenue.

Milestones:
- 7 consecutive days: 3% discount reward
- 14 consecutive days: 5% discount reward
- 30 consecutive days: 10% discount reward

Rules:
- a check-in is bound to the authenticated session/user identity;
- one qualifying check-in is credited per calendar day;
- streak breaks when the required interval is missed;
- rewards are recorded as entitlement events and may be redeemed against eligible purchases;
- discount issuance is not counted as revenue; downstream paid orders are.
