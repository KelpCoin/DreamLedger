# Truth Oracle — North Star

## What it is

A public **economic truth** surface: structured, evidence-oriented answers about prices, free/at-cost options, and commercial claims — closer to “Wikipedia for economic truths” than a blog.

Audience: **Kiwis first**, then others who want cheaper / fairer access to goods and services.

## What it is not

- Not a place to invent DreamLedger sales counts  
- Not a substitute for Stripe settlement proof  
- Not a dump of every silo’s inventory  

## Funnel (demand → stay → pay)

```
Truth Oracle (learn cheaper / free / at-cost)
    → trust + return visits          [Demand Sentinel: D-SEARCH, D-RETURN]
    → silo CTA cards                 [one primary CTA per silo]
        → avatar / cosmetics
        → billboard / DOOH
        → play (Phin Haven)
        → digital products
        → MTG / other silos
    → Intent-to-Pay Sentinel
    → live Stripe settle             [only then revenue]
```

## Content rules

1. Prefer **sources and method** over opinion  
2. Label uncertainty  
3. Free and at-cost options are first-class (builds demand without false scarcity)  
4. DreamLedger commercial CTAs are secondary and clearly marked as such  
5. Air-gap: articles and claim graphs can be authored offline; publish when connected  

## Relationship to Sentinels

Oracle queries and article reads emit **DemandNotes**.  
They do not emit IntentNotes unless the user moves to a checkout path.
