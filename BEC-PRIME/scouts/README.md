# BEC-PRIME Demand Scouts

The scouts are reconnaissance, not marketing bots.

They watch public infrastructure and public issue queues for repeated problems that can become a productized silo.

## Scout loop

1. Collect public signals.
2. Normalize the signal.
3. Cluster repeated problems.
4. Score demand, urgency, competition, build leverage, and payment proximity.
5. Produce a candidate silo.
6. Require human approval before activation, publication, or spending.

## What scouts do not do

- They do not post publicly.
- They do not contact prospects.
- They do not expose private BEC-PRIME implementation details.
- They do not unlock offers.
- They do not spend money.

## Expansion rule

Do not create a new website because an idea sounds interesting.

Create a new silo when an observed problem is repeated, has an identifiable buyer, has a clear paid outcome, and can reuse the existing BEC-PRIME commercial spine.

The desired output is:

signal -> evidence -> problem cluster -> offer candidate -> silo manifest -> compiler -> approval -> surface

This makes horizontal expansion a compilation problem rather than a bespoke development project.
