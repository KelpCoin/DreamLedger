# Agentic Commerce Surface

The active commercial wedge is AGENTIC-COMMERCE-READINESS-001.

The service assesses three observable dimensions:

- UNDERSTAND: can an agent correctly identify the merchant's offer?
- DECIDE: can an agent evaluate fit against shopper constraints?
- ACT: can an agent reach the intended purchase or enquiry action?

Commercial claims remain evidence-gated. No buyer, transaction, testimonial, ranking, scarcity claim, or revenue figure is considered real without underlying evidence.

External-facing output requires two substantive human review footprints before publication.

Initial target class: NZ merchants with high-value or high-consideration products.

Initial research target: PC Traders NZ.

The acquisition objective is a real paid audit, followed by documented delivery and permission-based proof.
