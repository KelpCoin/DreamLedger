# DreamLedger Public Boundary

Status: PUBLIC BOUNDARY NOTICE
Date: 2026-08-28

DreamLedger exposes customer-facing commerce surfaces, published products, and selected evidence. Internal implementation details are not part of the public product surface.

## Public

- Customer-facing product descriptions and prices.
- Published commerce routes and checkout paths.
- Public evidence and proof statements that are intentionally released.
- High-level product and service positioning where publication has been approved.

## Private

- Internal prompts and model instructions.
- Proprietary algorithms, scoring rules, decision policies, and compiler logic.
- Internal architecture diagrams and implementation details not explicitly approved for release.
- Customer data, operational data, private demand intelligence, credentials, tokens, and payment secrets.
- Internal experiments, unreleased offers, private pricing logic, and unpublished economic controls.
- Cross-silo implementation details that are not required by the customer-facing surface.

## Release rule

A public page must be understandable and useful without requiring disclosure of the private implementation behind it. Public evidence may describe what a product does and what has been verified, but must not disclose the machinery used to create, score, route, or govern private operations.

When in doubt, the material stays private until explicitly approved for publication.
