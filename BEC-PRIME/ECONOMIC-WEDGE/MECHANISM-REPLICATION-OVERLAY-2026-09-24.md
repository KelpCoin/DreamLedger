# Mechanism Replication Overlay
Date: 2026-09-24

Purpose: monetize repeatable market mechanisms without confusing pattern transfer with copying protected assets.

Rules:
1. Observe external demand and buyer behavior.
2. Extract the causal mechanism: buyer, trigger, offer shape, distribution surface, price, fulfillment, settlement, evidence.
3. Rebuild the mechanism from DreamLedger-owned assets and lawful/public inputs.
4. Do not copy proprietary code, private data, copyrighted creative output, credentials, or protected branding.
5. Run the rebuilt mechanism through Truth Oracle and Gauntlet.
6. Promote only after independent economic evidence.
7. Record failures as first-class evidence.
8. Replicate a mechanism into a second market only after the first cell has a verified causal chain.

Priority overlays:
- Marketplace overlay: meet buyers where existing demand already exists; browser-backed publication when APIs are unavailable.
- Agent-commerce overlay: expose a narrow payable endpoint where agents can discover and pay per call.
- Evidence overlay: sell verified information rather than generic content.
- Distribution overlay: reuse existing audiences, search surfaces, marketplaces and physical-to-digital QR pathways.
- Product overlay: convert observed recurring pain into a concrete paid artifact.

The objective is not to imitate a company. It is to isolate an economic mechanism, rebuild it with DreamLedger assets, measure the result, and retain only mechanisms that survive external verification.
