# BEC Beachheads

Purpose: turn existing DreamLedger surfaces into independently testable commercial beachheads.

## 1. Agentic Commerce Home
Offer: make a merchant discoverable, understandable and transactable by software agents.
MVP: public explainer, machine-readable offer catalog, readiness assessment, approval-gated checkout.
Revenue proof: real paid merchant transaction or paid readiness engagement.

## 2. MTG Commerce
Offer: verified MTG inventory and high-value collectible offers.
MVP: canonical SKU, inventory, price, checkout, fulfillment and settlement proof.
Revenue proof: real paid EDH_0001 transaction.

## 3. B2B Marketplace
Offer: approved business listings with structured commercial terms.
MVP: seller intake -> approval -> public listing -> checkout where explicitly enabled.
Revenue proof: verified paid marketplace transaction.

## 4. Kelplantis
Offer: playable digital-world content with a deterministic item/economy substrate.
MVP: runtime slice -> public build -> measured engagement -> approved monetization surface.
Revenue proof: verified paid transaction, not telemetry alone.

## Operating contract
Each beachhead must have one doorway, one concrete offer, one verification path and one economic proof artifact. A working page is not revenue. Public publication and irreversible commercial actions remain approval-gated.

## Worker loop
Discover -> build smallest viable surface -> verify -> measure -> improve -> kill or compound.
