# 777 Candidate: Education Construction Procurement Intelligence

Status: UNVERIFIED CANDIDATE

## Observed signal

Stats NZ reported 41,268 new dwellings consented in the year ended August 2026, up 21 percent year on year. Non-residential building consents totalled $8.9 billion, with education buildings at approximately $1.32 billion, up 9.3 percent year on year.

Primary source:
https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/building-consents-issued-august-2026/

## Economic gravity

Education construction is a procurement-heavy environment where project timing, bid preparation, supplier comparison, compliance, and cost certainty can create measurable commercial friction.

Te Waihanga's June 2026 Pipeline update reported:
- $290 billion total Pipeline value
- $192.8 billion with committed funding or a confirmed funding source
- $12.7 billion across 519 initiatives progressing through procurement
- approximately $17.5 billion expected to enter construction in the following 12 months

Primary source:
https://tewaihanga.govt.nz/the-pipeline/pipeline-snapshot

## Documented friction

The 2024 Ministerial Inquiry into School Property found that procurement processes add considerable time and cost to projects and described internal processes as time consuming and inefficient.

Primary source:
https://www.beehive.govt.nz/sites/default/files/2024-10/Report%20of%20the%20Ministerial%20Inquiry%20into%20School%20Property.pdf

## Buyer hypothesis

Candidate buyer:
Main contractors and construction firms preparing bids or supplier packages for education projects.

Not the Ministry of Education itself.

## Capability hypothesis

Normalize 2–5 subcontractor quotes arriving in different formats into one line-item-matched comparison with source evidence, assumptions, exclusions, and unresolved differences.

The product claim is a deterministic procurement artefact, not an AI claim.

## Commercial hypothesis

Outcome:
Reduce bid-preparation friction and expose quote inconsistencies before a tender decision.

Time:
A comparison should be produced within a defined short turnaround, subject to input quality.

Risk reversal:
Show source evidence and unresolved items rather than silently guessing.

Proof:
Use real quote files and return a reproducible comparison artefact.

Cost of inaction:
Manual comparison consumes bid-team time and leaves inconsistencies unresolved during a rising-cost procurement environment.

## Test

Run 5–10 offer/audience/message/surface variants against qualified contractor audiences.

Measure:

- request for a sample comparison
- paid diagnostic/order
- settled payment
- fulfilled artefact
- verified outcome
