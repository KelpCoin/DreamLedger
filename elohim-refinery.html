BEC-PRIME / GOVERNED REFINEMENT

Elohim Refinery.

The refinery is the candidate-to-offer preparation layer. It turns qualified signals and canonical assets into structured candidates, then hands them to deterministic gates. It is deliberately not a self-publishing machine.

Signal

A demand signal enters the refinement process with its commercial silo and provenance intact.

Refine

Proposer, critic, synthesizer and monetizer roles can improve the candidate while deterministic policy remains authoritative.

Gauntlet

The candidate must satisfy required commercial, proof, privacy, payment and silo-boundary checks.

Approval

A passing candidate becomes an internal draft. Public publishing, checkout activation and outreach remain approval-gated.

SIGNAL -> REFINE -> GAUNTLET -> APPROVED DRAFT -> HUMAN APPROVAL -> PUBLIC OFFER -> CHECKOUT -> EXTERNAL PAYMENT -> PROOF
Current execution policy: the refinery configuration explicitly disables auto-publish, auto-checkout, auto-outreach and automatic payment claims. A pass writes an internal draft only.
