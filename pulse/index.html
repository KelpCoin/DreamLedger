Signals are leads, not proof.

DreamLedger's research cycle looks for source-linked commercial problems that may justify a small, testable product or workflow. A headline or model-generated candidate does not establish buyer intent, permission to contact anyone, a successful sale, or a verified economic outcome.

What belongs in a useful signal

A source URL, publisher, observation time, stable deduplication key, specific buyer problem, uncertainty, and a low-cost validation step. When source retrieval fails, the system records the failure rather than interpreting it as zero demand.

Current publication boundary

This index does not invent a signal count or rank candidates without a fresh, source-bound artifact. Public pulse cards are promoted only when their exact artifact and source can be checked. Internal JSON receipts and model outputs are not public evidence of demand or revenue.
