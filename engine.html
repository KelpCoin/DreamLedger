CUBE Compiler

Transforms canonical product and offer definitions into executable commerce surfaces, allowing the kernel to be reused across independent silos.

Multi-LLM Refinement

Proposer, Critic, Monetizer and Synthesizer workers iterate on demand signals, offers and experiments instead of relying on one-shot generation.

Persistent Memory

Signals, experiments, outcomes and Fossil evidence become durable machine-readable history that can inform later iterations.

Browning Gauntlet

Adversarially attacks candidates for demand, margin, price, fulfilment, security, policy, evidence, duplication and operational risk before execution.

Commerce Spine

Connects signal, offer, exposure, checkout, payment, fulfilment and evidence. The payment event is the economic truth boundary.

Fossil Evidence

Records observed economic events. Generated checkout links are not revenue proof. Successful payment plus signed webhook evidence is.
