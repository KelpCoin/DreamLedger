# Figure Eight Governance Physics v1

The Figure Eight is a governed economic runtime.

Hard boundaries:

1. Stored data is not instruction authority.
2. Proposed knowledge is not canonical knowledge.
3. Canonical knowledge is not actionable authority.
4. Action authorization is not settlement.
5. Settlement is not attribution.
6. Attribution is not fulfillment.
7. Fulfillment is not independent verification.
8. No economic outcome becomes VERIFIED without an external buyer, settled payment, attribution, fulfillment, and independent verification.
9. No worker promotes its own fossil.
10. Economic outcomes never enter the knowledge promotion path.
11. Superseded facts are excluded by the read path.
12. SEO publication is a governed economic action, not an ungoverned content dump.

## SEO lane

SEO is part of CUBE acquisition, not a separate marketing system.

The machine may discover:
- search intents
- query clusters
- demand-backed topics
- comparison pages
- transactional landing pages
- informational pages that create qualified paths toward governed offers

The machine may not invent:
- search volume
- rankings
- buyer demand
- prices
- stock
- conversion claims
- testimonials
- external evidence

Every SEO opportunity must carry provenance and independent-source count.

Commercial or transactional SEO publication requires:
- verified evidence
- at least one independent source
- canonical URL
- release approval
- source references
- internal-link evidence
- supersession/freshness handling

The SEO lane feeds:

OBSERVATION
-> SEO OPPORTUNITY
-> PROPOSITION
-> RELEASE GATE
-> HUMAN APPROVAL
-> PUBLICATION
-> EXTERNAL RESPONSE
-> ATTRIBUTION
-> SETTLEMENT
-> LEARNING

SEO therefore becomes an inbound actuator for the Figure Eight rather than a content factory.
