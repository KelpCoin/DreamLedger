Should I Still Go?

A short decision pack for the moment when you've already agreed to an event and now you're wondering whether to actually attend.

Try four questions first:
  1. Do I actually want to go?
  2. Would going cost me more than it is worth?
  3. Am I avoiding it because of temporary nerves, or because I genuinely do not want to be there?
  4. If nobody else reacted badly, what would I choose?
