This is the machine.

BEC-PRIME is the sovereign commerce-kernel architecture behind DreamLedger. Its defining design separates cognition, deterministic policy, compilation, adversarial verification, commerce execution, evidence and independent commercial silos.

Public disclosure boundary: architecture and public design principles are exposed intentionally. Secrets, credentials, private customer data and internal operational tokens remain private. Public code is evidence of implementation, not a transfer of ownership.

Constitution

Operating rules, authority, approval gates, evidence requirements and silo separation. Agents may propose; deterministic policy decides what can execute.

CUBE Compiler

Canonical-to-surface transformation. One economic kernel can compile independent commerce worlds without merging catalogues or commercial identity.

Multi-LLM Refinement

Proposer, Critic, Monetizer and Synthesizer workers refine demand signals and offers. Probabilistic cognition remains subordinate to deterministic gates.

Persistent Economic Memory

Signals, experiments, decisions, outcomes and evidence become durable machine-readable history.

Browning Gauntlet

Adversarial verification tests commercial completeness, price, payment, proof, privacy, policy and silo integrity.

Commerce Spine

Signal -> offer -> exposure -> checkout -> payment -> fulfilment -> evidence. External payment remains the economic truth boundary.

Fossil Evidence

Observed economic events become durable proof. A checkout URL is not revenue.

Independent Silos

Dreamiez, Kelplantis, MTG and future worlds can share infrastructure while maintaining separate catalogues, state and commercial boundaries.

Demand Radar

Demand discovery and calibration turn external signals into candidates that can enter the refinement and Gauntlet pipeline.

Elohim Refinery

Candidate assets and offers are generated and refined before public exposure. Generation does not equal approval.

Truth Boundary

The system separates architecture, candidates, published surfaces and verified economic events. It refuses to turn claims into fake revenue.

Neutral Front Door

DreamLedger presents independent worlds while providing a shared trust and commerce surface.
