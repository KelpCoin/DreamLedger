This is the machine.

BEC-PRIME is the sovereign commerce-kernel architecture behind DreamLedger. The system is not a generic AI storefront. Its defining design is the separation of cognition, deterministic policy, compilation, adversarial verification, commerce execution, evidence and independent commercial silos.

Public disclosure boundary: this page exposes the architecture, design principles and economic loop intentionally. Secrets, credentials, private customer data and internal operational tokens are never published. Public code is evidence of implementation, not a transfer of ownership.

1. Constitution

Operating rules, trust boundaries, authority, approval gates, evidence requirements and silo separation. Agents may propose; deterministic policy decides what can execute.

2. CUBE Compiler

Canonical-to-surface transformation. One economic kernel can compile independent commerce worlds without merging their catalogues, customer state or commercial identity.

3. Multi-LLM Refinement

Proposer, Critic, Monetizer and Synthesizer workers refine demand signals, offers and experiments. Probabilistic cognition is subordinate to deterministic gates.

4. Persistent Economic Memory

Signals, experiments, decisions, outcomes and evidence become durable machine-readable history. Later experiments can learn from what actually happened.

5. Browning Gauntlet

An adversarial gate tests demand, price, margin, fulfilment, evidence, security, policy, duplication, prompt injection, operational risk and silo contamination.

6. Commerce Spine

Signal -> offer -> exposure -> checkout -> payment -> fulfilment -> evidence. The payment event is the economic truth boundary.

7. Fossil Evidence

Observed economic events become durable proof. A checkout URL is not revenue. A real payment plus a valid webhook-generated Fossil is revenue evidence.

8. Independent Silos

Dreamiez, Kelp Atlantis, MTG and future worlds can share infrastructure while maintaining separate catalogues, state, customers and commercial boundaries.

9. Demand Radar

Demand discovery and calibration turn external signals into candidates that can enter the refinement and Gauntlet pipeline instead of inventing products in a vacuum.

10. Elohim Refinery

Candidate assets and offers are generated and refined before public exposure. Generation does not equal approval. Canonical state must survive the verification boundary.

11. Truth Boundary

The system explicitly separates architecture, generated candidates, published surfaces and verified economic events. It refuses to turn code or claims into fake revenue.

12. Neutral Front Door

DreamLedger presents independent worlds rather than pretending one vertical is the whole company. The homepage is a directory and trust surface, not a dumping ground.

The design values

Truth over theatre. No revenue claim without payment evidence. Human authority where it matters. Publishing and irreversible economic actions can remain approval-gated. Machine leverage without machine sovereignty. LLMs generate and critique, but deterministic policy and verification remain authoritative. Silo integrity. Shared infrastructure is useful; unauthorised state leakage is not. Compounding memory. The machine should remember experiments and outcomes rather than regenerate blindly. One kernel, many surfaces. The same economic machinery should be able to compile independent worlds without collapsing them into one catalogue.
