Contact DreamLedger

For website bugs, broken links, product-page questions, or non-sensitive service issues, use the DreamLedger issue tracker. Include the public URL and what you expected to happen.

Privacy warning: the issue tracker is public. Do not post personal information, payment details, account credentials, private supplier quotes, or confidential business material.

Existing order or purchase inquiry: Email Dreamledger.org@gmail.com. Include the product or order reference, but never your full card number, credentials, or sensitive attachments. For confidential documents, ask about an appropriate intake method before sending them. An inquiry is not a confirmed stock reservation or accepted purchase.

For a security concern, read the security page first. Do not assume that an issue has been accepted as a support case or that a response-time commitment exists unless that is explicitly confirmed.
