What the report contains

  • A result for each agreed acceptance criterion: PASS, FAIL, NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW, or INCONCLUSIVE.

  • Links or artifact references supporting each finding.

How the review works

  1. You provide one accessible URL or bounded artifact and up to five criteria.
  2. The scope is checked for clarity and accessibility.
  3. Each criterion is assessed against available evidence. Missing evidence is not treated as a pass.
  4. The report records findings, sources, and limitations separately.

No claim of a turnaround time, signature, independent timestamp, or delivery is made unless the relevant process and evidence exist.
