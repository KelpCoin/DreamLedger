Trust boundary

Useful automation needs sharp edges.

DreamLedger keeps commercial claims separate from internal activity. A checkout start is not treated as revenue. Internal records are not treated as buyers. A generated result is not treated as proof merely because a model produced it.

  • Service access is scoped to the purchased boundary.
  • Customer payments do not grant private-system access.
  • Fulfilment is distinct from payment settlement.
  • Evidence is preserved separately from interpretation.
  • External consequential actions remain authorization-gated.
