Questions

Before you buy.

What does NZ$50 actually buy?

A standard 100 x 100 placement on the DreamLedger 3000 canvas, one title/label and one clickable destination URL. It does not include customer-supplied image upload.

Can I upload an image?

Yes, as a separate +NZ$100 image placement add-on. The image is reviewed by a human before publication and approval is not guaranteed.

How long does the space stay up?

Approved placements are intended to remain public until January 1, 3000, subject to the published content policy.

Is this physical DOOH?

No. It is a web-native public canvas inspired by useful DOOH creative and marketing principles. It is not represented as physical billboard inventory.

Do you guarantee traffic or sales?

No. The product is a permanent position on a finite public canvas. Traffic, impressions and conversions are not guaranteed.

What happens after payment?

The server records the paid reservation, then content goes through the human approval process before publication.
