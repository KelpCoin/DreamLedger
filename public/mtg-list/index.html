What happens after you send it?

The Happy Homarid team can review the request, confirm the seller's details and communicate the next steps. Submission does not guarantee publication, reserve an item or create a payment. Future automatic free accounts, searchable peer listings and buy/trade requests are under development.

Avoid private addresses, government IDs, card numbers and confidential documents in this form. For high-value cards, photograph the exact printing and provide a timestamped picture when requested.

Happy Homarid MTG / DreamLedger is an independent NZ Magic community initiative, not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast. Peer payment and shipping happen outside DreamLedger unless an individually verified product checkout is explicitly shown.
