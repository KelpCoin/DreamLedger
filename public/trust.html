Plain-language operating boundary

Trust is a chain of evidence.

DreamLedger distinguishes a published interface from a working external action, and a working action from a verified economic outcome. We do not treat model output, an internal receipt, traffic, a checkout page, or a successful HTTP response as proof of revenue.

What counts as verified?

01 · Real demandA grounded signal from an external source, with provenance.
02 · Real buyerAn independent buyer or counterparty, not a self-test.
03 · Authorized actionConsent, identity, payment, and scope checks appropriate to the action.
04 · External resultEvidence that the external system actually performed the requested action.
05 · Settled paymentAuthoritative payment-provider evidence tied to the intended offer.
06 · FulfillmentThe promised deliverable was produced and delivered.
07 · Independent verificationEvidence is linked end-to-end and contradictions are handled explicitly.

Status labels

VERIFIED means the required evidence chain has been checked. UNVERIFIED means evidence is incomplete or not independently confirmed. TEST and SIMULATED describe non-production evidence. STALE means the evidence may no longer describe the current state. CONTRADICTED means evidence conflicts. UNMATCHED means an event cannot yet be reliably attributed.

Current public claim boundary

Published pages and configured checkout surfaces are not proof of buyer demand or successful fulfillment. The current economic scoreboard is VERIFIED_EXTERNAL_REVENUE = NZ$0.00 until independently attributable settlement and fulfillment evidence establishes otherwise.

We do not publish fabricated customer logos, testimonials, inventory counts, certifications, uptime figures, or revenue claims. If a route fails, a listing cannot be verified, or a purchase path is not proven end-to-end, that limitation should remain visible until corrected.

Security and reporting

Never send passwords, API keys, webhook signing secrets, payment card data, or other credentials in a public issue. Report a reproducible public-page or product defect through the issue tracker and include the affected URL, approximate time, expected behavior, and observed behavior. For sensitive security findings, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting if enabled for the repository.
