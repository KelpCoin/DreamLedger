What makes this a marketplace?

Discovery is shared. Offers are structured. Agents can consume the catalog. Buyers retain authorization. Existing Stripe settlement and fulfillment remain the economic authority. B2B browsing is free; A2A usage is priced per capability and must disclose its unit and budget. Digital goods need a licence and versioned delivery receipt. DOOH inventory must be owner-authorized, and GPU work must be opt-in and isolated. The first objective is one independent buyer crossing the wallet, then repeatable fulfilment.
