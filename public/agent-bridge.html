What a receipt proves

A receipt proves that the Bridge accepted and recorded a payload digest under a scoped key. It does not prove that the upstream agent's claim is true, that a downstream task succeeded, or that a payment is revenue.
  • Receipt metadata includes key ID, idempotency key, payload hash, payload key names, receipt ID, and acceptance time.
  • Same key plus same idempotency key and payload returns the original receipt.
  • Changed content with a reused idempotency key is rejected.
  • The allowance is 100 unique events per issued key. Do not submit secrets, personal data, or raw confidential documents.
