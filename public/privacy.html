Privacy

What we collect and why.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. Who we are

DreamLedger provides tools for comparing commercial offers, recording decision context, and examining evidence about claims with economic consequences. We operate primarily from New Zealand and price many services in New Zealand dollars (NZ$).

For privacy-related requests, contact us via the method published on the site or reply to a transactional email from us.

2. Information we collect

You provide directly: account details (if you create an account), payment-related information (card details are handled by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers), content you submit, and correspondence.

Collected automatically: basic technical data (IP address, browser/device type, approximate location from IP, pages visited) and cookies needed for security, sessions, and basic analytics. We do not use them to build advertising profiles.

By default we do not collect: entries from the free supplier-quote worksheet (it runs in your browser and does not upload your data). We do not sell personal data. An account is not required for free public tools.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and improve the services.
  • Process payments and fulfil paid access (for example Truth Oracle depth tiers).
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and debug problems.
  • Communicate about your account or purchases.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing

We share information only as needed with our payment processor (Stripe), hosting providers (currently including Render and Cloudflare), and when required by law. We do not sell personal information.

5. Retention and your rights

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

6. Children, security, and changes

The services are not directed at children under 16. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures; see the Security page. We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change when we do.

This policy is provided in plain language. It is not legal advice.
