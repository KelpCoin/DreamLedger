DREAMLEDGER · MARKET
The experiment

Make physical commerce readable to agents.

DreamLedger is testing a simple proposition: a software agent should be able to discover a real product, understand the offer, obtain the buyer's authorization, initiate payment, observe fulfilment and produce independently checkable evidence of the economic event.

The loop

01

Discover

Machine-readable product, price, availability and terms.

02

Evaluate

Match the item against the buyer's actual constraints.

03

Authorize

The buyer controls what the agent may purchase and under what limits.

04

Pay

Use the merchant's legitimate checkout and payment rail.

05

Fulfil

The seller delivers the actual physical product.

06

Verify

Independent evidence establishes what happened rather than trusting the agent's claim.

What DreamLedger is not claiming

A machine-readable catalogue is not a completed transaction. A checkout URL is not payment. A payment receipt is not fulfilment. A seller assertion is not independent verification. DreamLedger's public meter remains NZ$0 until an external economic event satisfies the evidence contract.

Why start with physical collectibles?

A single trading card provides a bounded experiment: identifiable object, observable price, finite inventory, known buyer community and straightforward physical fulfilment. If the same transaction machinery survives a category change, the machinery is more important than the card.

Build once, generalize later

The objective is not to create a Magic-only marketplace. The objective is to find the smallest reusable transaction primitive that can later support other physical goods without rebuilding the economic control and evidence layer from scratch.

Test the commercial surface

The current marketplace exposes live offers and Stripe checkout. The next proof is external: a real buyer, real payment, real fulfilment and independent verification.
