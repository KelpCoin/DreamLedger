Help keep MTG welcoming

DreamLedger’s Magic silo is for buying, selling, and trading cards with as little friction and stress as possible. We’re inviting a small moderation team so this stays a safe, fair, and friendly place — especially for newer players.

Why we want moderators

  • Keep conversations respectful — no bullying, harassment, or pile-ons
  • Help people list clearly (condition, city, NZD) so deals stay simple
  • Spot spam and bad-faith behaviour early
  • Welcome new sellers and buyers so the hobby stays fun
  • Model the culture we want: calm, constructive, player-first

Submissions go to the DreamLedger operator via FormSubmit. We read every application. Thank you for helping keep Magic trading kind.
