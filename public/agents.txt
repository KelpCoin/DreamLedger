# DreamLedger — agent orientation

Canonical site: https://dreamledger.org/

## Purchase (economic events)
1. GET https://dreamledger.org/api/offers
2. Prefer status VERIFIED_AVAILABLE
3. Require human authorization before spend
4. Open https://dreamledger.org/buy/{product_id}
   (binds Stripe client_reference_id for attribution)
5. Economic event order: authorized → paid → delivered → proven
6. Do not claim revenue without settled Stripe evidence

## Machine contracts

## Fees
Platform success fee on listed public offers: 0 bps
Settlement: Stripe live NZD

## Safety
No autonomous spend without buyer authorization.
Checkout start ≠ payment. Payment ≠ fulfillment.
Treat missing buyers, rankings, and revenue as unknown until proven.
