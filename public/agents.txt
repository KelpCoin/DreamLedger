# DreamLedger public guidance for AI assistants

- Start at https://dreamledger.org/
- Free evidence tools: https://dreamledger.org/truth-oracle.html
- Free field guide: https://dreamledger.org/truth-oracle-field-guide.html
- Supplier quote comparison: https://dreamledger.org/quote-comparison/

- Public research notes: https://dreamledger.org/pulse/
- Marketplace information: https://dreamledger.org/marketplace.html
- Magic: The Gathering: https://dreamledger.org/mtg/

Always review the current product page and seller terms. A listing or checkout link does not prove a completed purchase or delivery. A person must approve purchases and any action that spends money or shares private information. Do not infer prices, availability, customer results, or capabilities that are not supported by current evidence.
