Practical guide

Use AI to compare offers, not skip the checks.

AI assistants can help discover products, organise supplier quotes, and summarise terms. The buyer should still confirm the exact offer, seller, total price, delivery conditions, and return terms before spending.

Before buying

  • Open the original product or supplier page.
  • Check the exact model, quantity, currency, and current price.
  • Look for delivery charges, taxes, exclusions, and return terms.
  • Require explicit human approval before a purchase.
  • Keep a receipt and confirmation of delivery.
