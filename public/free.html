The trust gap we are trying to close

DreamLedger is not claiming to have Trade Me's protections today. Trust has to be earned through observable behaviour.

  • clear seller and item records
  • condition and provenance evidence where available
  • payment records separated from claims about sales
  • explicit dispute and aftercare rules
  • public evidence of what the platform actually did

The goal is not to copy a marketplace's branding. It is to make trust inspectable.
