Agentic commerce in New Zealand: a practical guide

AI assistants can help people discover products, compare terms, and prepare a purchase. A useful buying process still needs clear human authority, reliable source information, and proof of what happened.

Before acting

  • Confirm the product, variant, quantity, currency, and seller.
  • Compare delivery, taxes, fees, returns, and exclusions where known.
  • Check current terms at the original source.
  • Require explicit human approval before spending money or sharing sensitive information.
  • Keep checkout, settled payment, delivery, and refund evidence distinct.

Do not treat an AI recommendation, click, checkout start, or supplier claim as proof of a completed transaction. Unknown information should remain unknown.
