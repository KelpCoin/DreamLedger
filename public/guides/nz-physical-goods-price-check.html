How do you compare the real cost of a physical product?

The cheapest sticker price is often not the cheapest delivered purchase. Compare the complete transaction.

1 · Item price

Compare the exact product, variant, printing and condition.

2 · Shipping

Add domestic or international shipping and any insurance required.

3 · Currency

Convert foreign prices to NZD using a current rate and include card or platform conversion costs where applicable.

4 · Taxes and fees

Consider applicable GST, duties and marketplace or payment charges.

5 · Availability

A cheaper listing that cannot actually be purchased is not a cheaper offer.

6 · Risk

Seller reputation, returns, condition evidence and delivery method have economic value.

For collectible goods

Exact identity matters. Two listings can have the same card or product name but different editions, conditions, languages, finishes or accessories. Compare the complete specification before comparing price.

For agentic buying

An agent should receive enough structured information to calculate delivered value rather than optimizing a single sticker-price field. Otherwise it can confidently select the wrong “deal.”
