What actually proves an online purchase happened?

Commerce systems often collapse several different claims into one green tick. They should not.

CLAIMED

An agent, user or seller says the transaction happened.

AUTHORIZED

A valid policy or buyer authority permitted the action.

EXECUTED

The system attempted the external action.

RECEIPTED

An external service provides evidence that an event occurred.

RECOMPUTABLE

A separate process has enough retained inputs and rules to reproduce the relevant claim.

INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED

A verifier not relying solely on the producer's assertion checks the evidence and reaches a verdict.

ECONOMICALLY VERIFIED

The event satisfies the actual economic contract: external buyer, settled payment, attributable transaction and fulfilled obligation.

Core rule: a signed message can prove who signed it. It cannot, by itself, prove that the underlying economic event was useful, fulfilled or independently true.
