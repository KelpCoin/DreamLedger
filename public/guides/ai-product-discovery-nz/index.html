AI product discovery: check the offer, not just the recommendation

AI can shorten the search for products and suppliers, but the recommendation is only as reliable as its sources and comparison method.

Five practical checks

  1. Open the original product or supplier page.
  2. Confirm the exact model, size, quantity, and version.
  3. Compare total cost, including delivery and taxes when available.
  4. Check stock, return conditions, and offer date.
  5. Look for missing details and conflicting evidence before committing.

A listed price may be stale. A product match may be approximate. A seller's claim is not an independent test. Keep those distinctions visible.
