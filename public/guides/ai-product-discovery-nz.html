How should a New Zealand seller make products discoverable to AI agents?

AI shopping changes the first storefront from a webpage a human reads to product information software can compare. The basics are surprisingly concrete.

Give every product an identity

Use a stable product ID, exact title, variant information, condition where relevant, current price, currency and stock status. Avoid changing identifiers just because the page design changed.

Keep price and checkout consistent

If structured information says one price and the checkout asks for another, an agent has a good reason to distrust the offer. The same SKU should resolve to one current commercial truth.

Expose the facts buyers actually compare

Shipping, location, returns, delivery estimates, stock and seller identity often matter more to a delegated buyer than marketing copy.

Answer questions, not only product names

Publish useful pages answering questions people ask before buying. A product page can convert someone who already knows you. An answer page can create the discovery path in the first place.

Machine discovery and human discovery reinforce each other: structured product facts help agents compare; useful explanatory pages help search engines and answer engines understand why the product matters.

Measure the actual funnel

Separate impressions, visits, product views, checkout starts, settled payments and fulfilled orders. Never treat a model mentioning your product as a sale.
