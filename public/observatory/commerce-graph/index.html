Compare offers on a fair basis.

Prices are only comparable when the underlying offer is comparable. A useful comparison records the item or service, variant, quantity, currency, delivery costs, taxes and fees where known, availability date, exclusions, and original source.

What a careful comparison should do

  • Keep the original source and the date it was checked.
  • Show missing delivery costs, taxes, stock, or return terms as unknown, not zero.
  • Do not rank unlike quantities, variants, or currencies as if they were equivalent.
  • Separate a supplier's own claim from independently confirmed information.
  • Distinguish an advertised price, a checkout, a completed purchase, and a settled transaction.

This page describes a comparison method. It does not claim that every supplier has been connected or that a live retailer integration is available.
