Facts you can check yourself.

Truth Oracle publishes claims only when the underlying evidence is identified. Dates matter. Primary sources matter. Calculations are labelled as calculations. If a figure cannot be verified, it stays out of the verified column.

Verification ruleVERIFIED means the source is identified, the observation date is recorded, and the claim can be independently checked. A model opinion is never primary evidence.
Verified · Employment · Effective 1 April 2026

Adult minimum wage

Hourly rate before tax
NZ$23.95

Employment New Zealand states that the adult minimum wage increased to NZ$23.95 per hour from 1 April 2026. The same official table gives NZ$958 for a 40-hour week before tax.

Primary source: Employment New Zealand

Verified · Transport · Week ending 6 September 2026

Regular 91 petrol board price

National weekly average board price
NZ$3.00 / litre

MBIE's latest published fuel-response page reports an actual weekly average board price of NZ$3.004568 for regular 91 petrol for the week ending 6 September 2026, rounded to NZ$3.00. This is the advertised board price, not necessarily the final price after discounts or loyalty schemes.

Primary source: MBIE

Method

No invented certainty

Truth Oracle deliberately does not publish the earlier unverified fuel and mortgage figures that circulated in drafts of this page. The current page contains only claims checked against primary sources available on the publication date.

For corrections, provide the source that contradicts a claim. A correction changes the public record and is itself recorded as an event.
