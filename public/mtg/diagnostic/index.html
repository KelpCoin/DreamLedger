Better Commander decks.
Zero paywalls.

Paste a complete 100-card Commander decklist to get a one-page structure report. No login, email, payment or subscription. The decklist is processed in your browser; card names are requested from Scryfall for metadata. Nothing is submitted to a DreamLedger account.

Analyse your deck

Include this commander as one card in your 100-card pasted list. Copy/paste imports from ManaBox, Moxfield or most plain-text deck exports.

What you get

  • PASS / QUARANTINE / FAIL structure verdict
  • Land count and average nonland mana value
  • Visible graveyard / Spirit synergy indicators, when relevant
  • Missing card metadata and uncertainty disclosed
  • Print or save your one-page report locally

Scryfall is an independent card-information service. Card names are sent to Scryfall to retrieve card types and mana values; their service may be unavailable. No pricing, card legality, tournament performance or gameplay win rate is assessed.

Not a complete simulation. A PASS means only these limited structural checks passed. A QUARANTINE result means missing inputs, metadata or unassessed synergy. Response time depends on Scryfall; no under-60-second delivery guarantee is made.
