The game plan

Priest of Titania and Elvish Archdruid help generate mana. Lys Alana Huntmaster and Imperious Perfect keep the board growing, while Voice of Many helps refill your hand.

Dwynen, Gilt-Leaf Daen gives your other Elves +1/+1 and gains you life when she attacks, based on the number of attacking Elves. The plan is to build a wide board, swing hard, and rebuild after removal.
