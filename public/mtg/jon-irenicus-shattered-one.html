The table becomes your weapon

You’re not necessarily attacking. You’re not necessarily committing much to the board. But somehow, everyone else is taking damage, missing plays, or stuck managing something you handed them. That part is absolutely necessary.

You don’t rush this deck. You set up as normal, draw, lands, maybe Phyrexian Arena ticking along. Then Jon lands, and from that point on every creature has a destination.

Read the table. Choose the gift.

  • Who’s ahead? Give them something restrictive.
  • Who’s vulnerable? Give them something dangerous.
  • Who just stabilised? Break it again.

Switcheroo, Shifting Loyalties and Modify Memory mean nothing stays solved. Assault Suit and Grafted Wargear make combat decisions awkward after the handoff. Crystal Shard lets you pull something back and send it somewhere worse.

You’re not closing the game yourself. You’re making sure nobody else gets to play well enough to close it either.
