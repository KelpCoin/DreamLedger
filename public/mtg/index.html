A safer place to trade starts with clear facts.

Peer deals are not paid to DreamLedger. Request real front/back card photos, confirm card condition and shipping or meetup terms, use a payment method with appropriate protection, and never treat an inquiry as a reserved or paid item. Personal seller details must not appear in public listings without consent.

Quick answers

Before your first trade.

Is it free to list Magic cards?

Preparing a listing request costs nothing. The proposed peer marketplace has a 0% DreamLedger success fee. Listings still need review before appearing publicly; there is not yet an automatic multi-seller publishing system. Independent payment or shipping charges may still apply between peers.

Can I buy a Commander deck?

You can review the listed Commander decks below. EDH_0001 and other decks are inquiry-first until pricing, availability, reservation and payment fulfilment are verified. These are Happy Homarid inventory, not community seller listings.

Where is the MTG forum?

The discussion hub describes the planned forum rooms. For existing community participation visit Happy Homarid on Patreon. Access to free memberships, comments and community chats is set by the creator.

Is this an official Magic: The Gathering website?

No. Happy Homarid MTG / DreamLedger is an independent fan and marketplace initiative, not endorsed by Wizards of the Coast or Hasbro.
