The two-party trade ledger is in development

Word is bond, with a record both people agree to.

We're building a DreamLedger flow where two signed-in traders review identical card, printing, condition, quantity and handoff terms and each confirms the same agreement. The aim is a shared record of what was agreed, not escrow or guaranteed delivery.

Not live yet. For now, agree and organise your trades in the Happy Homarid Discord. Discord conversations do not automatically create a DreamLedger trade ledger or provide purchase protection.
