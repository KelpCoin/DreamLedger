Transaction rules

Stock and seller identity must pass verification before auction publication. Server-locked bids are immutable, and the clock closes by server time. Winning creates an order, not a charge. Stripe payment must match the exact order, currency and amount. Dispatch requires seller proof; the buyer can confirm receipt or open a time-limited dispute. No automatic seller payouts are enabled.
