What this evidence means

The hash-linked record documents agreement and self-reported handoff events. It does not itself prove card authenticity, payment, physical delivery, ownership or legal finality. A disputed trade may still be challenged under applicable New Zealand law.

Independent tamper-resistant anchoring is not yet active. A visible hash alone cannot prove that the entire private history has not been rewritten or truncated.
