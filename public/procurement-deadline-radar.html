qualification law

When does the NZ$25K surface appear?

VALUE_CONFIRMED or VALUE_RANGE_CONFIRMED + economic object ≥ NZ$10M + buyer-capacity evidence + urgency + sufficient evidence depth. Until those conditions are met, the event remains a NZ$49 route. No contract value is inferred from title, agency, tender type, or project appearance.

Truth boundary: UNVERIFIED. GETS establishes the public procurement event. It does not establish buyer intent, contract value, award probability, payment, fulfillment, or revenue.
