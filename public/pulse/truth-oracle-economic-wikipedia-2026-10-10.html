The Truth Oracle as an economic Wikipedia

A public, source-linked map of economic claims, evidence, tools, commercial opportunities and the systems that can act on them. The asset is not a pile of generated pages. It is a growing network of claims that can be checked, corrected, reused and connected to useful work.

Architecture and component map Revenue not implied

How the knowledge compounds

Each expedition should preserve the source snapshot, its publication date, the exact claim extracted, a stable source URL, a content hash where available, the evidence that supports or contradicts the claim, the review state, and links to related claims and tools. Corrections create a dated successor rather than silently overwriting history. A source being present is not the same as its claim being verified; a verified fact is not the same as buyer demand; and buyer demand is not revenue.

Free public layer

Searchable claims, source links, contradiction notes, freshness labels, reusable checklists, and clear explanations of what is known, unknown, stale, or disputed. Readers should be able to inspect the evidence without paying or creating an account where privacy and source terms permit.

Paid evidence layer

Where a buyer needs more than the public verdict, paid tiers can provide a source bundle, structured comparison, audit trail, export, monitoring, or a scoped human-reviewed deliverable. Each tier must have a distinct deliverable, disclosed limits, an existing approved price and checkout, and a tested fulfillment path. The free worksheet remains free; a paid evidence service must not be disguised as the worksheet.

Agent Bridge and A2A

Agents discover capabilities and machine-readable schemas for free where practical. Charge only for a real metered service, validated result, compute consumption, or completed delivery. Bind authorization to the exact capability, price, expiry and budget; record idempotent settlement and delivery receipts. Model output or an internal job is not a completed paid task.

Reusable component factory

Promote proven stencils for evidence receipts, catalogues, checkout handoffs, entitlements, avatar cosmetics, billboard inventory, APIs and accessible mobile interfaces. Each stencil carries provenance, license, dependencies, tests, supported routes, failure modes, and the proof required before reuse. Do not clone an unverified component into many silos.

Existing public components to connect

  • Truth Oracle: free evidence tools and the public claim-checking entry point.
  • DreamMeez Studio: the avatar identity and cosmetics surface. A visible studio is not yet proof of paid cosmetic entitlement or fulfillment.
  • The Internet Billboard: digital placement and inventory. Inventory, review, payment, and publication states must remain distinct.
  • Agent Bridge: governed handoff for agent capabilities and commerce.
  • Marketplace: discovery entry point. A listing is not available for purchase until its offer, seller/availability, checkout and delivery are verified.
  • Agentic commerce blueprint: free B2B discovery, low-cost A2A, reusable digital goods, and permissioned compute as separate capabilities.

One router, many silos

New products should be data-driven routes and silo manifests on the existing hosting substrate, not a new Render service for every idea. A candidate manifest needs an owner, canonical path, one primary CTA, source and license record, data boundaries, checkout/fulfillment contract, route tests, HTTP status, canonical URL, DNS/TLS verification, rollback target and a kill switch. A route that returns 200 is necessary but not sufficient: its links, payment handoff, entitlement, and delivery must also work.

What 777 must improve on every expedition

  1. Inspect the current repository, open issues, recent artifacts and available run evidence before creating anything.
  2. Prefer one existing component with a clear commercial gap over a new unrelated concept.
  3. Generate a source-backed, non-duplicate artifact or implement a bounded component improvement; include tests and update the index.
  4. Run the existing gates and record the run ID, commit, route checks, and failures. A green workflow proves only the checks it actually ran.
  5. Keep the economic truth chain separate: independent buyer, authorized action, externally settled and attributable payment, durable order/entitlement, fulfillment, delivery evidence, reconciliation.
  6. Promote only components that pass provenance, security, accessibility, privacy, license, and rollback checks. Let failed candidates die small.

Current evidence boundary: this page is a reusable public architecture artifact, not evidence of traffic, search indexing, customer demand, a paid order, GPU utilization, or revenue. Those outcomes require their own external measurements.
