Durable component blueprint · 10 October 2026

Four economic components, one evidence standard.

A decomposable execution map for 777 blocks 301–400. The aim is not to launch four heavyweight platforms at once. It is to build small, reusable proof-producing components, validate one narrow job, and expand only when a buyer or repeatable demand signal is evidenced.

Agent Bridge compatible Revenue-first Unknown ≠ pass

Evidence boundary: the market facts and figures in the originating research brief are leads, not independently verified findings in this artifact. Before publishing a factual market claim or activating a regulated workflow, capture the primary source, date, jurisdiction, exact passage, counterevidence and review state. This page does not claim a buyer, live integration, regulatory approval, trade, or revenue.

Component portfolio

301–325 · Aerospace provenance

Part evidence receipt

Job: turn supplied component and release documents into a traceable evidence packet. Start with document indexing, field extraction, provenance, missing-field checks and export, not airworthiness certification.

Potential buyer: maintenance, repair and overhaul teams or parts distributors. First sellable deliverable: a bounded document review with an explicit human approval step.

Required evidence: authoritative aviation rules and acceptable-document guidance for the target jurisdiction; source files and permissions; extraction accuracy tests; audit trail; liability and non-certification disclaimer.

Gate: never state a part is airworthy or approved based solely on model output.

326–350 · Carbon forwards

Bilateral matching preparation

Job: structure buyer and seller intent, delivery period, volume, price basis, eligibility, counterparty constraints and due-diligence status into a match-ready brief.

Potential buyer: carbon-market participants or specialist intermediaries. First sellable deliverable: a private, non-binding matching brief and evidence-backed shortlist, before any transaction fee model.

Required evidence: current registry and market rules, authoritative price data with timestamp, unit definitions, contract terms, counterparty consent, and legal review of brokerage/intermediation requirements.

Gate: no anonymous trade execution, custody, or claim of a match until identity, authority, eligibility and settlement controls are designed and approved.

351–375 · Cold-chain evidence

Shipment evidence receipt

Job: normalize supplied temperature checkpoints and device metadata into a timestamped, tamper-evident report with gaps and excursions clearly shown.

Potential buyer: logistics operators, product owners, insurers or quality teams. First sellable deliverable: a one-shipment evidence pack using customer-provided exports, with no new hardware dependency.

Required evidence: device provenance, calibration status, time-zone handling, chain of custody, retention policy, privacy terms and a validated rule set for the specific shipment.

Gate: a hash proves file integrity after capture, not that a sensor was accurate or the original data was truthful. Do not imply GDP compliance or product safety without appropriate validation.

376–400 · Customs discrepancy review

Entry audit and refund evidence pack

Job: compare an import entry against the documents and rules supplied for a specific jurisdiction; flag mismatches, cite source passages and assemble a reviewer-ready packet.

Potential buyer: importers, brokers or trade-compliance teams. First sellable deliverable: a fixed-scope retrospective review of a small batch of entries, with findings approved by a qualified human.

Required evidence: current tariff and customs sources, entry data, jurisdiction/date of entry, confidence and counterevidence, reviewer sign-off, and a transparent calculation of any potential recovery.

Gate: do not promise refunds or charge contingency fees until legal eligibility, client authority, calculation and actual recovery are verified.

Shared component contract

  1. Ingest: accept only authorized sources and customer files; retain the source URL or file identity, timestamp, jurisdiction and content hash where feasible.
  2. Normalize: transform into a typed schema with required fields, units, currencies, timezone, provenance and explicit null/unknown states.
  3. Evaluate: run deterministic checks first; use candidate evaluator for bounded synthesis and Gauntlet for adversarial checks. Missing evidence stays missing, not green.
  4. Produce: emit a durable HTML/JSON artifact, a machine-readable schema, a list of unresolved questions and a versioned receipt.
  5. Commercialize: expose a free discovery or preview surface; charge only for a specific export, reviewed deliverable, metered API or completed job that has a real fulfillment path.
  6. Prove: connect the authorized buyer, exact offer, external payment settlement, fulfillment and delivery evidence. Workflow success and model output do not count as revenue.

Agent Bridge execution contract

  • Capabilities are declared with input/output schema, permissions, price or budget, timeout, data handling, idempotency key and version.
  • Discovery can be free. A2A charges should be low, explicit and tied to measured use or completed delivery, not arbitrary access fees.
  • Every job moves through PROPOSED → AUTHORIZED → RUNNING → EVIDENCE_READY → EVALUATED → DELIVERED, with separate payment and refund states. Failure and dispute states are first-class.
  • Workers receive least-privilege, time-limited access. No arbitrary public access to the user's GPU: isolate workloads, cap CPU/GPU/RAM/time/network, block secrets and host filesystem, and require explicit opt-in before exposing capacity.
  • Local LM Studio/llmster execution is a candidate route, not a verified runtime until a real worker receipt, model identifier, resource measurement and artifact hash are captured.

How 777 should break this down

  1. Expedition 1: retrieve primary sources for each vertical and create a claim ledger with VERIFIED / UNVERIFIED / CONTRADICTED / STALE labels. Do not publish unsupported market-size numbers.
  2. Expedition 2: identify one narrow document-in/document-out task per vertical and produce schema fixtures plus adversarial tests.
  3. Expedition 3: choose the lowest-friction component that can be delivered without regulated execution or new hardware. Build a free sample and a clear paid deliverable using an existing, verified checkout rail.
  4. Expedition 4: connect Agent Bridge receipts, entitlement and delivery evidence; test idempotency, retries, refunds, duplicate webhooks and failure handling.
  5. Expedition 5: measure real buyer intent, conversion, fulfillment time, error rate and repeat use. Kill small if no demand; scale only after external proof.

Acceptance criteria

  • One source-linked, non-duplicate public artifact added to the observatory index.
  • One versioned input/output schema and test fixture for the selected component.
  • No fabricated market statistics, buyer counts, integration status or revenue claims.
  • Mobile-readable page and links to Truth Oracle, DreamMeez, Billboard and Agent Bridge.
  • Run receipt records workflow run ID, commit, artifact paths, tests and truth status when those facts are actually available.
