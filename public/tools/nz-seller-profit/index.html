The calculation

Profit = sale price − item cost − platform fee − payment fee − fixed payment fee − shipping − packaging.

Margin = profit ÷ sale price. Return on item cost = profit ÷ item cost. Taxes, refunds, returns, insurance and other business costs are not included unless you add them to the relevant inputs.

Why sellers use this

  • A sale can look profitable until fees and postage are included.
  • Comparing the profit from two selling channels is easier when the same costs are entered for each.
  • For high-value inventory, a small percentage fee can change the result by a meaningful amount.
