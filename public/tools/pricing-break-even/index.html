This is a planning calculation, not accounting, tax or financial advice. It assumes fixed costs stay fixed and variable cost scales with each sale.

How it works

Contribution per sale = selling price − variable cost. Break-even sales = fixed costs ÷ contribution per sale. Estimated monthly profit = (selling price − variable cost) × sales − fixed costs.

For a target profit, required price = variable cost + (fixed costs + target profit) ÷ expected sales.

These formulas make the result inspectable rather than hiding the calculation behind a black box.

Why this matters

  • A low price can require an unrealistic number of sales.
  • A higher price does not automatically mean higher profit if it materially reduces sales.
  • The useful question is not simply “What should I charge?” but “What price and sales volume make the economics work?”
