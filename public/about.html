ABOUT DREAMLEDGER

Useful systems.
Clear terms.

DreamLedger is a small digital commerce and service surface built around bounded, evidence-backed work. The primary service is Automation Rescue: a one-time service for diagnosing, repairing, testing and documenting one clearly defined automation failure.

The wider system can surface other digital offers, but each offer has its own scope, price and fulfilment path.

How we operate

  • Prices and deliverables are stated before purchase.
  • One-time offers do not become subscriptions by default.
  • Payment is recorded only when the payment provider confirms it.
  • Public outreach and publication remain approval-gated.
  • Claims are separated from evidence rather than dressed up as certainty.
