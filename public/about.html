About

Practical tools. Honest limits.

DreamLedger builds tools for comparing commercial offers, organising source information, and checking claims before decisions are made. The aim is simple: make useful details easier to see and unknowns harder to miss.

How we work

We favour clear product descriptions, traceable sources, and capabilities that can be checked. Examples are labelled as examples. Prices, availability, and conditions should be checked on the relevant product page. We do not invent missing information or promise a result that has not been demonstrated.
