ABOUT DREAMLEDGER 3000

Many surfaces.
One front door.

DreamLedger is a multi-surface home for digital products, public placements, avatar worlds, collectibles, utilities and experiments. The job of the front door is simple: make the ecosystem legible, make real offers easy to find, and keep each world on its own terms.

What lives here

  • Billboard: the DreamLedger 3000 founding placement, currently offered as a NZ$50 100×100 web tile with human review.
  • DreamMeez: the avatar and garden-facing world, including identity and collectible surfaces.
  • Magic: Commander diagnostics, physical decks and collectible-card inventory.
  • Truth Oracle and tools: evidence, utilities and automation-facing surfaces kept separate from consumer inventory.

How commerce works

Every offer has its own price, scope, checkout and fulfilment path. A page view is not a sale. Payment, delivery and evidence are separate states, and gated products are not presented as available just because a catalogue record exists.

What DreamLedger does not promise

The site does not manufacture audience numbers, impressions, conversions, scarcity timers or technical certainty. Where a product is live, its terms are stated. Where a surface is still being built, the site should say so.
