Then turn unused stuff into money

The point is not to make you browse another giant catalogue. It is to help you identify one thing you already own, establish what a sensible sale would look like, prepare it properly, and eventually trade through a transaction path with evidence.

1. Identify

Find something you no longer need.

2. Prepare

Capture condition, provenance and the facts a buyer needs.

3. Price

Understand the likely economics before listing.

4. Trade

Use a legitimate payment and delivery path with a recorded transaction trail.

Trust is earned by records

DreamLedger will not manufacture ratings, buyers, protection or transaction history. When a real trade happens, the system can record the evidence needed to distinguish an actual completed transaction from an internal claim.
