PUBLIC STATUS SNAPSHOT

Service status

A plain-language record of what is available, what is deliberately disabled, and what has not yet been proven. We do not label an untested money flow as operational.

Snapshot reviewed: 11 October 2026
Monitoring level: Manually reviewed snapshot, not live telemetry. No uptime guarantee or SLA is implied.

Public website and shop

Available

The public storefront and static offer fallback are served. A product-listing script may affect dynamic catalogue details; fallback offers remain visible.

Toll Road catalogue

Available

The public manifest and 10,000 logical road identifiers are available. Catalogue presence does not mean a road has been sold or executed.

Paid Toll Road execution

Disabled by design

Paid checkout and metered execution remain disabled until durable per-key quota accounting and end-to-end payment tests are in place. No paid-call availability is promised.

Payments and fulfilment

Not yet independently verified

A complete external settlement → attributable order → fulfilment → delivery-evidence cycle has not yet been established as verified. Do not treat a checkout link or payment attempt as proof of delivery.

MTG and B2B/A2A marketplace

In development

Public discovery and some offer pages exist. A fully durable listing-to-order-to-settlement-to-fulfilment marketplace is not yet available as a verified end-to-end service.

Persistent usage ledger

Not ready for billing

Production metering is gated until usage can be recorded durably and enforced per customer key. Metered calls stay disabled rather than risking unbounded or unprovable usage.
