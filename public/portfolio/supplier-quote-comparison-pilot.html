Provide your existing written supplier quotes. The proposed fixed-scope pilot turns them into one evidence-backed comparison so your team can inspect total costs, quantities, delivery, exclusions and unresolved risks.

NOT ACCEPTING PURCHASES. This is an internal commercial validation preview, not an approved or deliverable checkout offer. NZ$2,500 is a proposed pilot price to test with eligible buyers, not a validated market price or an active invoice.

Proposed scope of one pilot

  • One purchasing decision, up to five buyer-provided supplier quotes in readable PDF, CSV or text.
  • Normalised side-by-side comparison of quoted totals, currencies, shipping, lead times, minimum order quantities, terms and exclusions where the sources actually state them.
  • Evidence links or page references for every material extracted field; disagreements and absent details flagged as UNKNOWN, not filled by AI guesswork.
  • One exportable decision table (CSV) and one written decision packet (PDF or accessible HTML), with one bounded factual correction pass.

What is explicitly excluded

We do not promise supplier outreach, negotiations, legal or financial advice, automatically selecting a vendor, saving a guaranteed amount of money, unsanctioned scraping, live ERP integration or ongoing managed consulting. The buyer remains responsible for its purchasing decision.

Acceptance gates before anyone can pay

Confirmed access rights to source files, a successful end-to-end sample, an agreed delivery date and price, secure handling of confidential quotes, a working payment and delivery path, and a named person responsible for support and exceptions. Failure of any gate keeps checkout disabled.
