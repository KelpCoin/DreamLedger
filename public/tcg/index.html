A better trade starts before the money changes hands

Get the details right

Check exact set, print, language, condition and quantity. Use recent photos of the actual card, including its back.

Agree on NZ delivery

Confirm price in NZD, postage, tracking, packaging, and who carries transit risk. Meet in a safe public place if trading locally.

Protect both sides

Use payment methods with appropriate buyer protection. Never treat an enquiry or a saved draft as a reservation or completed order.
