DREAMLEDGER LAUNCH STORY

Working title:
BUILD ONE. PROVE IT. COPY WHAT WORKS.

Core story:
DreamLedger is an experiment in building a system that can discover commercial opportunities, turn them into concrete offers, distribute them, fulfill them, measure the result and learn which mechanisms deserve replication.

The point is not to pretend that an autonomous business already exists at scale.

The point is to make the first real transaction, preserve the evidence, and use the transaction itself as the instruction for what to build next.
