Automation Rescue

Automation Rescue is a NZ$99 one-time service for one existing automation and one bounded failure path. The service covers reproduction, root-cause diagnosis, focused repair, basic testing and a concise repair record.

Typical delivery is within 24–48 hours for work that fits the stated scope. Larger rebuilds, unrelated failures and new systems are outside the bounded offer and are not silently added to the job.

Payment

Checkout is processed by Stripe. DreamLedger does not treat a click, checkout session or abandoned payment as revenue. A purchase is recorded after payment confirmation.

Refunds and scope

If a request is materially outside the stated service scope, it will not be represented as an in-scope repair. Scope questions should be resolved before work begins. Any refund decision is handled against the actual purchase and the circumstances of the request rather than an invented blanket promise.

Digital fulfilment

After a confirmed purchase, the fulfilment flow provides the customer with the appropriate submission entry point. Submitted material should be limited to what is necessary for the agreed repair.
