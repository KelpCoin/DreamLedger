Am I overpaying?

Start with your current bill or quote, then compare the exact service, usage, fees, conditions, and date. A lower headline price is not always a lower total cost.

Common places to check

Power and electricityCompare usage, daily charges, discounts, contract terms, and exit fees.
Broadband and mobileCheck data limits, speed, modem costs, coverage, and introductory pricing.
Groceries and fuelCompare like-for-like quantities and record the shop or station and date.
Insurance and bankingCompare excesses, exclusions, recurring fees, and what is actually covered.

Five checks before switching

  1. Save the original bill, quote, or provider terms.
  2. Record the date and exact plan or product.
  3. Compare the total cost for your actual usage.
  4. Check discounts, expiry dates, exclusions, and cancellation fees.
  5. Keep unknown or missing details visible instead of guessing.

A claim can be true but irrelevant to your situation. Check whether the comparison answers your actual question.
