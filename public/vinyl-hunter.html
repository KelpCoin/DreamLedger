Vinyl Grail Hunter

Find the record you want. Then test the market for a real opportunity.

This is an evidence-first hunting surface. A listing price is an asking price, not a completed sale. The system will not call something a bargain without a current observable listing, pressing confidence and landed-cost arithmetic.

How it works

  1. Build a wanted list of exact pressings.
  2. Collect current listings from supported marketplaces.
  3. Normalize price, shipping, condition and pressing identity.
  4. Calculate landed cost.
  5. Flag candidates below your ceiling.
  6. Keep the purchase itself outside the system until explicit authority exists.

Turn it into a service

Vinyl Grail Hunt: NZ$29 prepared offer. Buyer supplies up to a bounded wanted list. Fulfilment is a human-reviewed candidate report. Checkout is intentionally not opened until the fulfilment loop is proven.

Current source rails include Discogs marketplace data, Trade Me, and eBay. Discogs exposes marketplace listings and price suggestions; Trade Me exposes authenticated seller-side sold-item data; eBay's public API surface is more constrained for historical sold data, so sold evidence must be treated separately from active asks.
