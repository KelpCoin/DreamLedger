What this tool does and does not do

Does: extracts supplier name, stated total, recognizable currency, explicit tax basis, MOQ, lead time and payment terms. It only ranks when all quotes have totals, a recognizable matching currency, and an explicit matching tax basis.

Does not: calculate landed cost, freight, duties or exchange rates, verify suppliers, or replace professional advice. Unknowns remain missing. Ranking uses stated totals only, not scope or total landed cost.
