FREE NZ COST-OF-LIVING GUIDE

Check your power bill before you accept it.

Power prices rose materially in 2026. You do not need to guess whether your current plan is competitive. Start with your actual bill and the Electricity Authority's free comparison service.

Fast route
  1. Find your latest bill and note your retailer, plan, usage and meter type.
  2. Use the Electricity Authority's Billy comparison service to compare plans available to you.
  3. Read the terms before switching. Check fixed-term break fees, connection requirements and any special conditions.
  4. Consider time-of-use only if your household can genuinely shift usage into cheaper periods.
