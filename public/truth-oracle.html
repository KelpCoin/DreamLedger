Free evidence tools (always on)

1. Write the claim

One sentence. Who benefits if it is believed?

2. List sources

URL, date, what it actually supports — not what you hope it supports.

3. Mark unknowns

Missing data stays missing. Do not fill gaps with confidence.

4. Verdict language

Supported / contradicted / insufficient evidence. Avoid false certainty.

CLAIM:
SOURCE 1 (url, date, supports):
SOURCE 2:
UNKNOWN / NOT CHECKED:
VERDICT: supported | contradicted | insufficient
CONFIDENCE: low | medium | high
PAYMENT INFLUENCE: none (required)

Editorial and commercial boundaries

Progressive transparency

Opinions are labelled as opinions; sourced facts link to evidence; unknowns stay visible. Payment must never change an evidence verdict or override a failed check.

Economic truth

A listing is not a sale. Telemetry is not revenue. Only settled payment + fulfillment + independent evidence supports VERIFIED economic claims.

Gauntlet / Elohim

Elohim may propose scorecards and candidates. The gauntlet judges. Neither may declare verified revenue without external evidence.
