EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

Evidence is not the same thing as a claim.

OBSERVED, INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED, SOURCE SUPPORT, DERIVED, INFERRED, CONTRADICTED and UNKNOWN are distinct states. A source is not automatically an observation. Evidence can change. Contradictions stay visible.
