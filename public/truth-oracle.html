Review evidence method

Free tools (always on)

1. Write the claim

One sentence. Who benefits if it is believed?

2. List sources

URL, date, what it actually supports — not what you hope it supports.

3. Mark unknowns

Missing data stays missing. Do not fill gaps with confidence.

4. Verdict language

Supported / contradicted / insufficient evidence. Avoid false certainty.

CLAIM:
SOURCE 1 (url, date, supports):
SOURCE 2:
UNKNOWN / NOT CHECKED:
VERDICT: supported | contradicted | insufficient
CONFIDENCE: low | medium | high
PAYMENT INFLUENCE: none (required)

Hard rules

Progressive transparency

Higher tiers buy time, packaging, and delivery. They do not buy a greener light. A paid report can still return FAIL or NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW.

Economic truth

A listing is not a sale. Telemetry is not revenue. Only settled payment + fulfillment + independent evidence supports VERIFIED economic claims.

Source-based assessments

Automated checks can identify contradictions and missing evidence; no automated claim or internal process can declare a sale verified without independent external proof.
