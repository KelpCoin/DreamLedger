How review works

Structured review

What a review covers

When the Review tier is open, you supply one public URL or bounded file and up to five clear criteria. The result is a structured record — not a guarantee that claims in the artifact are true worldwide.

In scope

One accessible public source or file; criteria written so a reviewer can decide what evidence would satisfy each one.

Out of scope

Private systems without access, unlimited criteria, legal or financial advice, or any promise that payment improves the verdict.

Outcome labels

Each criterion is marked pass, fail, or needs human review, with the supporting passage or the gap explained.

Free field guide

Five checks before trusting a commercial claim

1. Find the original source.

Prefer source documents, official terms, and underlying data over reposts.

2. Check the date and scope.

Record when it was published and which market, version, or group it covers.

3. Separate fact from interpretation.

Quote the exact evidence. Label estimates and opinions separately.

4. Look for disconfirming evidence.

Search for exclusions, corrections, and independent confirmation.

5. Match evidence to the decision.

A claim can be true but irrelevant. Check what happens if it is wrong.
