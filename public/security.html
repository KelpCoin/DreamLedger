Security

How we protect the service.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. Principles

  • We prefer clear boundaries over confident claims.
  • Payment card data is handled by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Free browser-side tools are designed so that your entries are not uploaded by the tool itself.
  • Public evidence surfaces are intentionally limited; protected implementation details stay protected.

2. Transport and headers

The public site is served over HTTPS. Current response headers include Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS), Content-Security-Policy, X-Frame-Options: DENY, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, and Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin.

3. Infrastructure and payments

The public storefront is served via Cloudflare and Render. Paid products use Stripe Checkout. Stripe is responsible for card processing under its own programme.

4. Vulnerability reporting

If you believe you have found a security issue:

  • Do not publicly disclose exploit details before we have had a reasonable chance to respond.
  • Send a clear description (affected URL, steps to reproduce, potential impact) via the site contact method or GitHub security reporting.
  • Do not include passwords, live API keys, or real customer payment data.

We do not currently operate a formal bug-bounty programme with guaranteed payouts.

5. What this page does not claim

  • We do not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, or equivalent certification at this time.
  • We do not claim the service is free of vulnerabilities.
  • We do not claim enterprise-grade uptime SLAs unless a specific paid agreement states otherwise.
