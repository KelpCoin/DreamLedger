BEC-PRIME Intellectual Property Surface

This page is the public-readable expression of the existing BEC-PRIME design language and economic control model. It distinguishes system concepts from live economic evidence.

Constitution

Reality outranks generated claims. Generated is not approved. Approved is not sold. Sold is not payment. Payment becomes Level 1 economic evidence only after a verified payment event and fulfillment evidence.

CUBE Compiler

The compiler turns canonical economic and asset definitions into machine-readable offers, runtime surfaces, and verification artifacts. Surfaces are outputs of a controlled source rather than disconnected marketing pages.

Cognitive and Economic Spine

  • Multi-LLM refinement: proposer, critic, synthesizer, monetizer.
  • Demand Radar: discovery and demand signals.
  • Elohim Refinery: refinement of candidate economic outputs.
  • Persistent Economic Memory: durable learning from verified outcomes.
  • Revenue OS: demand, offer, exposure, conversion, fulfillment, feedback and ledger stages.

Gauntlet and Truth Boundary

The Browning Gauntlet is the deterministic gate. The Truth Boundary prevents simulated, inferred, or generated events from being represented as economic proof.

Commerce Spine and Fossil Evidence

The commerce path is DEMAND -> REFINE -> GAUNTLET -> COMPILE -> DISTRIBUTE -> CHECKOUT -> PAYMENT -> FOSSIL -> MEMORY -> ITERATE. A Fossil is created only from verified payment evidence.

Independent Silos

Dreamiez, Kelplantis, MTG and BEC-PRIME are separate tunnels. Shared assets may interoperate through a canonical shared asset contract, but catalogues, offers, permissions and economic evidence do not bleed between silos.

Neutral Front Door

The front door is a thumb-first horizontal discovery surface. It is a routing layer, not a generic storefront. Each card enters an isolated silo tunnel.

Humaniser and Approval Governor

The Humaniser Gate keeps generated output coherent for people. The Approval Governor controls what can become a live economic offer. An empty approval registry means checkout-enabled offers must be zero.

Digital Proxy and Silo Integrity

Digital proxies may operate within defined permissions. They cannot bypass approval, truth, payment verification, or silo boundaries.
