# Monetization Autopilot Operating Contract

Status: ACTIVE

## North Star

Optimize for legitimate, compounding revenue with minimal human cognitive load.

The machine is the factory, not the product.

Core loop:

DEMAND -> OFFER -> CUSTOMER -> PAYMENT -> FULFILMENT -> EVIDENCE -> TRUTH ORACLE -> VERIFIED REVENUE -> REPEAT

## Economic truth rules

- EXTERNAL_CUSTOMER is required for Level 2/3 economic proof.
- Self-payment, test cards, internal transfers, and refund loops never count as external revenue.
- Missing payment reference, delivery evidence, or complete fossil means FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE.
- Architecture, CI, or deployment success never overrides missing economic evidence.
- Revenue claims remain NZ$0 until independently evidenced.

## Control planes

### Truth Oracle
Canonical economic adjudicator. It determines PASS, FAIL, or INCONCLUSIVE from evidence rather than architecture claims.

### Gauntlet
Adversarial evidence and experiment gate. Rejects weak, contaminated, incomplete, or non-reproducible claims.

### Elohim
Evidence refinement layer. Improves proposals and evidence packages while preserving provenance and never manufacturing evidence.

### CI/CD
Every commercial change should compile, verify, enforce silo boundaries, run the ecosystem gate, and emit machine-readable proof before promotion.

## Automation policy

The machine should autonomously handle low-risk discovery, scoring, clustering, compilation, verification, evidence processing, monitoring, ledgering, reporting, and deterministic fulfilment.

Human approval remains required for material spending, strategic pivots, sensitive external actions, public actions not explicitly authorized, and irreversible legal/reputational decisions.

Public posting remains approval-gated by default.

## Revenue engine

1. Ingest legitimate demand signals.
2. Normalize and score signals.
3. Identify commercially testable opportunities.
4. Run the opportunity through Gauntlet.
5. Use Elohim to refine the evidence-backed proposal.
6. Instantiate the smallest viable offer using existing commerce infrastructure.
7. Route genuine customer payments through the canonical checkout.
8. Record the economic event and independent ledger evidence.
9. Fulfil the order and record delivery evidence.
10. Fossilise the complete trail under a unique run_id.
11. Run Truth Oracle adjudication.
12. Scale only validated winners.
13. Quarantine failed or contaminated experiments.

## Human interface

The desired operating model is near-zero routine intervention.

Daily briefing should contain only:

- verified revenue
- pending revenue
- strongest current opportunity
- blockers
- Truth Oracle verdicts
- system health
- decisions requiring Biggie or Peggy

No architecture essays in the daily briefing.

## Commercial constraints

- No fabricated scarcity.
- No fake social proof.
- No fabricated testimonials.
- No deceptive claims.
- No manufactured economic events.
- No cross-silo contamination.
- Real scarcity may be used when factually true and verifiable.
- Relevant, truthful persuasion and low-friction checkout are permitted.

## Silo policy

MTG / HappyHomarid / CollectorsCoast remain separated from Amplissa/adult.

Commercial data, offers, customer evidence, and automation state must not leak across silos.

## Scaling policy

Successful experiments become candidates for automatic replication only after economic validation and within configured risk/budget limits.

Failed experiments are automatically quarantined with preserved evidence.

Revenue declines trigger investigation, not blind spending.

Budget breaches trigger automatic stop/quarantine.
