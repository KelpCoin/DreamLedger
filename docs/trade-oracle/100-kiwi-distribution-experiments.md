## Operating rule

Do not run all 100. Select small experiments and measure:

real buyer intent -> response -> payment -> fulfillment -> verified outcome.

Prioritise channels where people are already asking to buy, source, fix, price, value, or diagnose something. A view, click, like, or prepared post is not revenue.

### First five experiments

1. NZ Market Lag of the Week: publish one evidence-backed discrepancy.
2. Trade Me: test one highly specific fixed-price diagnostic.
3. High-intent community mining: find people already asking for the relevant help.
4. Local-business margin scan: offer a concrete diagnostic to a small set of businesses.
5. QR/physical distribution: place a small number of legitimate cards or flyers where relevant.

Success condition: first stranger payment, correct fulfillment, independent verification, and an economic Fossil. Only then automate or replicate the winning distribution behavior.
