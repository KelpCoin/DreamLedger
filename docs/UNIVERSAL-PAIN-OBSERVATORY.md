## Strategic thesis

The product target is not "a marketplace clone". The target is a transaction operating system for businesses: discovery -> qualification -> quote/RFQ -> trust -> agreement -> payment -> fulfilment -> evidence -> repeat.

The universal-pain strategy is to find problems that recur across countries, industries and company sizes, then choose a narrow wedge where a buyer can pay for an objectively measurable outcome. The global layer comes from the underlying pain; the initial wedge provides tractability.

## Product selection rule

Score each candidate on:
- universality
- frequency
- pain severity
- money at risk
- time saved
- urgency
- digital solvability
- objective proofability
- repeatability
- automation potential
- trust requirement
- existing distribution
- identifiable buyer
- concrete deliverable
- willingness to pay
- human minutes per transaction
- cross-border portability
- adjacency to marketplace transactions

Do not choose a winner by intuition. Validate the smallest wedge with a real buyer and a real transaction.

## B2B Trade Me direction

Trade Me's current Marketplace already covers listings, auctions/Buy Now, professional seller tooling, payments and shipping. Its current payment model emphasizes Ping and Buyer Protection, while professional sellers also have listing tools, invoicing and shipping workflows. Therefore a credible competitor should not merely reproduce listing pages. The opening is to reduce the operational friction surrounding B2B trade: RFQ, structured catalog data, supplier verification, quote normalization, procurement, payment/settlement, shipping, acceptance, disputes, evidence and repeat purchasing.

The product should be marketplace + transaction OS, not marketplace-only.

## Economic gate

UNVERIFIED problem -> observed demand -> qualified buyer -> smallest paid offer -> authorized transaction -> settled payment -> fulfilment -> independent evidence -> repeat transaction -> automation -> replication.

Traffic, listings, registrations, AI calls, internal rows and checkout starts are not revenue.

## First product experiments

A. B2B RFQ normalizer: buyer uploads requirements and supplier quotes; system produces a comparable decision packet.
B. Transaction passport: one record connecting listing, buyer/seller identity, agreement, payment, shipping, acceptance and evidence.
C. Surplus exchange: businesses publish idle equipment/material and buyers discover verified matches.
D. Procurement preflight: compare quote, landed cost, supplier evidence, terms and policy before purchase.
E. Settlement/reconciliation rail: match orders, fees, refunds, payouts and invoices.

Each experiment is a hypothesis until an external buyer pays.

## Operating law

Search broadly. Validate narrowly. Build only after evidence. Automate only after a transaction works. Replicate only after it repeats.

## Product-generation law

Universal does not mean generic. Start with a specific buyer and painful transaction, then expand through the underlying invariant. The preferred invariant is:

**messy real-world input -> verified structure -> decision -> transaction -> external result -> evidence -> repeatability**

A candidate is promoted only when a real buyer, real payment, real fulfilment and independent evidence exist. Internal activity remains non-economic.

## B2B marketplace thesis

Trade Me's current Marketplace already integrates listing, payments and buyer protection, and its professional seller tooling includes listing management, reporting, invoicing and shipping workflows. The competitor opportunity therefore should not be a superficial listing clone. The research target is the layer around B2B transactions: structured discovery, RFQ, supplier/buyer trust, quote normalization, procurement controls, settlement, delivery, acceptance, disputes, reconciliation and repeat purchasing. Trade Me documents Ping as its standard Marketplace payment method, with a 2.19% seller transaction fee and Buyer Protection up to $5,000; professional sellers retain a separate commercial fee structure. These current facts are reference inputs, not assumptions about an eventual competitor's economics.

## Universal product factory

The 300-vector corpus now spans transaction friction, finance, procurement, logistics, identity, compliance, property, field service, workforce, information management, household administration and recurring commerce.

The factory rule is deliberately stricter than "build 300 silos":

1. A pain vector is a research candidate, not a product.
2. A candidate becomes a product hypothesis when a specific buyer, input, output and transaction boundary can be named.
3. A product becomes a sellable silo only when DreamLedger can fulfil the promised output with existing capability, at a known cost, without pretending that automation exists where it does not.
4. A live public silo requires a real route, public page, exact offer, fulfilment contract, payment path or explicit human-gated purchase path, evidence boundary, health check and CI/deployment verification.
5. A paid silo is not economically verified until an independent buyer pays, the payment settles, the contracted work is fulfilled and the evidence chain is independently recorded.
6. Candidate silos must never be marked live merely because a page, Stripe link, database row or internal workflow exists.
7. The swarm may discover, qualify, prepare, test and monitor. It does not manufacture demand or count internal activity as money.
8. Build order is determined by real buyer access, transaction density, fulfilment capability, proofability and human-minute cost, not by how interesting the problem sounds.
9. A clone is useful only when the underlying transaction primitive is reusable. Clone the mechanism, not the false evidence.
10. The first reusable mechanism for the Trade Me direction is: requirement -> structured RFQ -> quote normalization -> counterparty evidence -> decision -> transaction -> delivery/acceptance -> reconciliation -> repeat.

## Silo readiness contract

A silo is READY only when all are true:

- public URL resolves to the intended surface;
- route is present in the canonical public router;
- offer has a unique product/offer identifier;
- price and currency are explicit;
- buyer inputs are explicit;
- fulfilment capability exists and is testable;
- payment path is real if payment is advertised;
- human gates are explicit;
- evidence output is defined;
- health check passes;
- CI/CD has a relevant successful run;
- no claim on the public surface exceeds verified evidence.

Otherwise the silo remains CANDIDATE, PREPARED, GATED or BLOCKED.
