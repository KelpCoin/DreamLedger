# Commerce platform build target

Shopify combines checkout, payments, inventory, customers, POS, omnichannel selling, reporting and back-office operations. DreamLedger is not at parity yet.

## Build layers
- multi-tenant stores
- products, variants, pricing and inventory
- cart and checkout
- payment provider adapters
- orders, refunds and returns
- customers and accounts
- fulfillment and digital delivery
- discounts and gift cards
- taxes and shipping
- analytics and reporting
- staff roles and permissions
- webhooks and integrations
- themes and customization
- marketplace/app ecosystem

## Differentiating layer
- evidence-linked transactions
- settlement reconciliation
- contradiction detection
- decision objects
- MCP access
- governed agent actions
- auditable approval gates

## Enterprise direction
Palantir models data, logic, actions and security as an operational ontology. UiPath emphasizes durable orchestration across agents, robots, APIs, legacy UIs and people. DreamLedger should evolve into a commerce operating system with a governed object/action/evidence layer.

Never claim parity until implemented and tested.
