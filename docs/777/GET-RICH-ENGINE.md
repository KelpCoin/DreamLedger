# DreamLedger 777 Get-Rich Engine

The 777 loop continuously searches for a shortest credible path from zero verified external revenue to repeatable external revenue.

It is reality-first:
- no invented buyers
- no invented payments
- no self-purchases
- no simulated revenue
- no fabricated fulfillment
- no unauthorized outreach
- no movement of the canonical economic scoreboard without independent external evidence

Search sequence:

WORLD / PUBLIC DEMAND SIGNALS
→ EXISTING DREAMLEDGER CAPABILITIES
→ OPPORTUNITY
→ FULFILLMENT GATE
→ PAYMENT PATH
→ EXTERNAL EVIDENCE
→ REPEATABILITY
→ AUTOMATION
→ SCALE

The workflow also runs combinatorial 777 hypothesis generation against existing economic primitives. Generated candidates remain HYPOTHESIS_UNVERIFIED until independently tested.

Public market signals are research inputs only. They are not proof of willingness to pay.

The economic scoreboard remains:
