5. Commerce Spine: offer registry -> checkout -> payment settlement -> fulfillment -> durable evidence.

8. Silo Isolation: MTG and other commercial surfaces remain independently bounded so capabilities and data do not cross-contaminate.
9. Approval Governor: public distribution and irreversible actions remain explicitly gated where required.
10. Atom Engine: small executable economic actions with explicit success/failure conditions and proof requirements.
