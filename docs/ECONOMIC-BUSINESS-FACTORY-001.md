# Economic Business Factory 001

Status: INTERNAL PREPARATION
External actions: HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED
Revenue truth: VERIFIED_EXTERNAL_REVENUE remains NZ$0.00 until an independent settled buyer payment and fulfillment evidence exist.

## Objective

Convert observed economic demand into repeatable, evidence-gated businesses by composing existing CUBE, Elohim, Gauntlet, Truth Oracle, offer, fulfillment, payment, attribution, and learning components.

The factory does not manufacture fake businesses. It manufactures candidate economic cells and execution packets, then promotes only cells that survive evidence and outcome gates.

## Factory loop

WORLD -> DEMAND -> QUALIFY -> CAPABILITY -> OFFER -> DISTRIBUTION -> INTENT -> PAYMENT -> FULFILLMENT -> VERIFIED OUTCOME -> UNIT ECONOMICS -> REPLICATION

Each stage emits a durable artifact and a machine-checkable state transition.

## Cell contract

Each candidate business cell must contain:

- buyer: specific buyer class and problem
- evidence: source references and evidence tier
- offer: exact deliverable, price hypothesis, and scope
- capability: named fulfillment capability and health state
- distribution: at least one reachable channel
- attribution: deterministic campaign/source identifier
- payment: live payment rail or explicit blocked state
- fulfillment: acceptance criteria and evidence plan
- kill rules: explicit conditions for stopping
- learning: predicted outcome and later observed outcome

## Promotion gates

1. DISCOVERED: observed demand exists.
2. QUALIFIED: buyer/problem/scope fit survives adversarial review.
3. READY: capability and fulfillment contract are available.
4. APPROVAL_REQUIRED: external effect requires human authority.
5. EXPOSED: an approved external distribution event has occurred.
6. INTENT: a real external person has demonstrated purchase intent.
7. PAID: payment is independently observed and settled.
8. FULFILLED: contracted deliverable is actually delivered.
9. VERIFIED: payment, attribution, fulfillment, and evidence reconcile.
10. REPLICABLE: repeatability evidence exists across independent transactions.

A candidate cannot skip states merely because an upstream model predicts success.

## Factory scaling rule

Do not scale by creating thousands of untested offers. Scale by cloning a proven mechanism across independent buyer/problem cells while inheriting only verified mechanics. Evidence, buyer identity, demand claims, pricing claims, and outcomes do not inherit automatically.

Replication requires a source cell with verified outcome evidence plus a mutation definition. Every child cell remains independently gated.

## Attention minimization

The machine may autonomously perform internal discovery, normalization, qualification preparation, evidence collection, packet construction, reconciliation, and learning. Human attention is reserved for external publication, outreach, live financial actions, credentials, and ambiguous high-risk decisions.

## Free-first distribution policy

Before paid acquisition, test zero-cost or already-authorized channels: public sample pages, permitted directories, editorial submissions, community/resource placements where authorized, organic search, and inbound agent-readable endpoints. Do not purchase distribution until organic evidence demonstrates a conversion problem worth paying to solve.

## Factory success condition

The factory is successful only when it produces at least one independently paid and fulfilled transaction from a cell without manual reconstruction of the entire pipeline, and the resulting evidence is sufficient to reproduce the cell with materially less human attention.

## Failure conditions

Kill or quarantine a cell when:

- primary evidence cannot support the core claim
- buyer intent is inferred rather than observed
- the payment path is unavailable or irreconcilable
- fulfillment cannot be completed within the stated contract
- attribution cannot distinguish the cell's external outcome
- unit economics remain below the configured floor after observed costs
- repeated independent attempts produce no meaningful buyer signal

## Factory telemetry

Measure at minimum:

The governing metric is verified external outcomes per unit of human attention, not internal activity volume.
