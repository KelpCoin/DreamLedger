777 is the replication law connecting the existing DreamLedger/BEC substrate. It is not a new product, agent, database, or revenue claim.

The machine continuously converts economically interesting real-world substrate into bounded cells, tests those cells against external reality, records verified mechanisms, and returns only eligible mechanisms to CUBE for controlled replication.

## Authority boundaries

### CUBE
Compiles substrate into bounded economic cells.

CUBE may classify, allocate, isolate, compose, mutate, and queue candidates.

CUBE may not declare revenue or verified economic success.

### Supabase
Machine-facing home of silo and cell state, execution state, evidence relationships, telemetry, authorization state, and economic records.

Supabase is the runtime record, not independent economic reality.

### Supervisor
Allocates available compute and coordinates CUBE and swarm work.

The Supervisor may delegate GPU capacity and reconcile state. It may not manufacture economic truth or bypass authorization.

### LM Studio / BECK local models
Local reasoning and generation substrate.

Workers may research, normalize, create, repair, score, compare, and prepare artifacts. They may not self-authorize public/live-money/irreversible actions or promote UNVERIFIED state.

### Swarm
Bounded workers operating under Supervisor allocation.

Workers return artifacts, receipts, evidence references, and failure states.

### Elohim
Creates, refines, repairs, and proposes.

Elohim also creates/refines the scorecard used by Gauntlet for the relevant cell.

Elohim is not the final judge and cannot promote its own work.

### Gauntlet
Adversarial release authority.

Gauntlet judges Elohim output against the current evidence, freshness, contradiction state, feasibility, attribution path, fulfillment path, and configured kill conditions.

PASS means eligible for the next authorized gate. PASS does not mean successful economics.

### Authorization boundary
Consequential external action requires the configured authorization policy.

Human approval remains required for public/live-money/irreversible/platform-required actions unless a separately authorized execution policy explicitly permits the action.

### Truth Oracle
Independent economic reality verifier.

Truth Oracle verifies provenance, freshness, contradiction state, buyer independence, attribution, settlement, fulfillment, and independent evidence.

Truth Oracle is the promotion boundary for economic truth.

### BECK ledger
Immutable internal record of verified mechanisms and their supporting evidence.

BECK records what Truth Oracle has established. It does not replace Truth Oracle.

## Evergreen expansion

A verified mechanism is not copied as a guaranteed winner.

After verification:

1. attempt the configured repeatability gate;
2. measure fulfillment effort, contribution margin, conversion, and repeat behaviour;
3. derive 5-10 controlled replicas when the bounded test plan permits;
4. vary one or more dimensions such as audience, channel, packaging, price, proposition, or fulfillment wrapper;
5. instrument every replica;
6. support observed survivors;
7. kill or hold non-survivors;
8. return surviving mechanisms to CUBE as verified substrate.

Replication copies the mechanism demonstrated by reality, not the brand name.
