# Autonomous Business Factory

Status: ACTIVE
Date: 2026-09-24 NZST

Purpose

Operate the existing BrownEye Cortex / DreamLedger infrastructure as a portfolio of small autonomous economic loops rather than as a single product.

Operating loop

signal -> offer -> feasibility -> package -> authorized distribution -> order/payment -> automated fulfillment -> independent verification -> reconcile -> learn -> repeat -> replicate

Design principles

1. Reuse existing infrastructure. Do not build a second platform when an existing commerce cell, checkout, fulfillment path, CUBE signal, Elohim proposal, Gauntlet gate, Truth Oracle evidence path, Stripe rail, or Supabase state can perform the job.
2. Optimize for external economic events, not internal activity.
3. Prefer products and services with digital or mechanical fulfillment, clear pricing, low marginal founder effort, repeat-purchase potential, and machine-readable distribution.
4. Maintain multiple independent loops so one failed offer does not stop the portfolio.
5. A winning loop becomes a mechanism that can be cloned into adjacent evidence-backed opportunities.
6. Failures mutate the smallest broken component. No architecture expansion without evidence.
7. Human attention is reserved for taste, approvals, exceptions, credentials, and irreversible external actions.
8. Never count database rows, payment links, clicks, checkout sessions, test charges, simulated transactions, or model output as revenue.
9. Revenue becomes VERIFIED only after independent external settlement, attribution, fulfillment, and evidence.
10. All products must remain legal and not be aimed at children.

Portfolio cells

- CMD-DIAG-29: production-ready commerce cell; first conversion experiment.
- Existing MTG offers: reusable domain-specific commerce mechanisms and inventory paths.
- Billboard offers: existing low-friction digital/advertising cell.
- C2 Decision Analysis: candidate digital reasoning product pending independent checkout/fulfillment verification.
- Other archived/draft offers remain dormant until evidence supports activation.

Autonomy boundary

The system may autonomously research, score, package, test internally, repair safe internal state, deploy safe changes, reconcile external payments, fulfill settled orders, and improve existing loops.

External publication, outreach, spending, charging, refunds, and other irreversible external effects remain approval-gated unless an existing explicit authorization permits them.

Success condition

The factory is successful when multiple cells can repeatedly progress from real demand signal to settled payment to automated fulfillment to independently verified evidence with little or no founder intervention.
