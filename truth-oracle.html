02 / Fuel

Does the cheaper station really save?

Use the current pump prices from your fuel app, then account for how far you need to detour.

Check Gaspy prices ↗

Estimate only. Use pump prices, not stale sign prices, and include the entire extra drive. Does not include the value of your time or traffic.

03 / Electricity

Compare the whole power year

Use your actual bill and offer details. This estimate compares a single-rate usage model with fixed charges and credits; it is not a substitute for a personalised power quotation.

Billy · public power comparator ↗Powerswitch ↗

Positive annual credits reduce cost; negative values add cost. Include credits only if you qualify, and check contract exit fees, time-of-use requirements, bundled services and any rate changes.

04 / Mobile, broadband, gas & other bills

12-month all-in cost checker

Compare the current bill with an alternative after temporary deals, monthly discounts, setup costs and credits expire. Use actual contract details, not just a headline monthly price.

Mobile plan comparison ↗Broadband Compare ↗

Use for mobile, internet, bottled/natural gas or other recurring services. Include only credits you can actually claim. An advertised discount is not a saving if other costs rise.

Truth Oracle standard

A saving claim should survive a cross-examination

Source it

Keep the original URL, date checked, plan name, unit rate, promotion terms and eligibility rules.

Compare like for like

Normalize product quantities, include ongoing prices and fees, and separate an estimate from a confirmed quote.

Keep uncertainty visible

If the offer is unavailable at your address, a credit is conditional, or the price is stale, record that instead of guessing.
