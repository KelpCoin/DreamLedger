Five checks before trusting a commercial claim

Use this before acting on a price, supplier statement, product claim, performance promise, or business opportunity.

1. Find the original source.

Prefer the source document, official terms, product page, filing, or direct statement over a repost or summary.

2. Check the date and scope.

Confirm when the information was published, which market or version it covers, and whether the terms remain current.

3. Separate fact from interpretation.

Quote the exact evidence. Label estimates, opinions, forecasts, and inferences separately.

4. Look for disconfirming evidence.

Search for exclusions, corrections, counterexamples, conflicting numbers, and independent confirmation.

5. Match the evidence to the decision.

A claim can be true but irrelevant. Check whether it answers the actual question and what happens if it is wrong.

Educational guidance, not legal, financial, medical, or procurement advice. Verify important claims with suitable primary sources.
