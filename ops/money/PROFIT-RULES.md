# Profit rules (hard)

Verified revenue is still **NZ$0** until external pay. These rules stop the business becoming busy and broke.

## Price floors (do not break)

| SKU | Floor NZD | Why |
|-----|-----------|-----|
| Commander diagnostic | **29** | Below this, report time destroys margin |
| Billboard tile | **50** | Scarcity + review cost |
| Discord kit | **79** | Anchor digital margin product |
| Builder stack (diag+kit) | **99** | Must beat sum discount carefully |
| Founder pack (tile+kit) | **129** | Premium AOV |

No “mates rates” that train the market to wait for discounts.

## COGS caps (time)

| SKU | Max fulfil time |
|-----|-----------------|
| Kit | **15 min** (file send / wall key) |
| Diagnostic | **45 min** (use template) |
| Tile | **20 min** review + place |

If a job exceeds cap → improve template, don’t silently eat hours.

## Scarcity

- Founding tiles: treat as **limited**; don’t dump unlimited free clones  
- Prefer paid tile over free “branding” giveaways  

## Sell order (profit)

1. Close **any** first pay ($29 OK)  
2. Every post after that leads with **$79 kit** or **$99+ bundle**  
3. Ads only after organic pay + fulfil under COGS cap  

## Forbidden

- Building new SKUs before 3 paid fulfilments  
- Discounting kit below $79  
- Counting care/play as revenue
