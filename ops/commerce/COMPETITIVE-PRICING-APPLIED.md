# Competitive pricing models — applied to DreamLedger

**Applied policy (not a brainstorm).** Live offers stay in `approved.json` until operator changes Stripe links.

## Models considered

| Model | Idea | Fit |
|-------|------|-----|
| Cost-plus | Cost + margin | Weak for digital; costs near zero marginal |
| Value-based | Price to outcome | Strong for diagnostic + tile permanence |
| Competitor-matched | Match Trade Me / Shopify fees narrative | We undercut **platform fees** (0 bps story), not global ad CPMs |
| Good-better-best | Tier ladder | Apply as **candidate tiers**, not 50 live SKUs |
| Psychological | $49 vs $50 | Tile already NZ$50 round; keep |
| Anchor + decoy | High anchor makes mid look fair | Optional later bundle |

## Applied decisions

### Founding Tile NZ$50 (LIVE)

- **Keep NZ$50.** Round number, low cognitive load, scarcity is canvas not fake countdown.  
- Competitive frame: permanent web real-estate vs ephemeral social ads — do not claim traffic.  
- Image add-on NZ$100 already in offer — **keep** as premium friction (human review = real cost).  
- Do **not** race to NZ$19 tile; destroys founding story and increases junk content load.

### Commander Diagnostic NZ$29 (LIVE)

- **Keep NZ$29.** Below casual “consulting hour,” above impulse trash.  
- Competitor mental model: human coach NZ$50–150; automated report undercuts with instant delivery.  
- Passive path: Performance Wall + existing report route — husband does not re-analyse each deck if automation holds.

### Good-better-best (CANDIDATES only)

| Tier | Price NZD | Role |
|------|-----------|------|
| Good | 29 | Diagnostic alone |
| Better | 50 | Tile alone |
| Best | 69–79 | Bundle tile+diag (candidate) |

No third live plink until first fossil exists.

### Passive digital pack pricing

Default **NZ$9 / 19 / 29** ladder for wall-delivered PDFs so husband is not negotiating per sale.

## Anti-patterns rejected

- Dynamic surge pricing (overhead)  
- Per-buyer haggling  
- 200 simultaneous live prices  
- Free tier that consumes support
