# FIGHTEDGE product contract

Public surfaces may expose verified facts, attributed analysis, market observations, and clearly labelled hypotheses.

Paid surfaces may package deeper research, source triangulation, market snapshots, contradiction maps, analyst ledgers, and post-event audits.

No product may imply guaranteed profit, guaranteed winning selections, or fabricated historical performance.

Revenue proof remains separate from content performance. A paid subscription or sale counts as external economic truth only after an independent buyer and settled payment are verified.

Public release is approval-gated.
